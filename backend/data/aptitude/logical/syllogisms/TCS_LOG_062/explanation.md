# Question TCS_LOG_062 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Neither I nor II follows)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Syllogisms

### Question
All pens are pencils. Some pencils are erasers. Conclusions: (I) Some pens are erasers. (II) All erasers are pens.

### Options
- (A) Neither I nor II follows
- (B) Only I follows
- (C) Only II follows
- (D) Both follow

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Syllogisms, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Neither I nor II follows" is the valid solution.
