# Question TCS_LOG_172 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Statement I is the cause, Statement II is the effect)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Cause and Effect

### Question
Statement I: Fuel prices rose sharply this month. Statement II: Public transport usage increased significantly. Relation:

### Options
- (A) Statement I is the cause, Statement II is the effect
- (B) Statement II is the cause, Statement I is the effect
- (C) Both are independent
- (D) Cannot determine

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Cause and Effect, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Statement I is the cause, Statement II is the effect" is the valid solution.
