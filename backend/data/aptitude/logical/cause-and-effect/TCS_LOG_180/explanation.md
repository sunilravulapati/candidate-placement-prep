# Question TCS_LOG_180 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (II is cause, I is effect)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Cause and Effect

### Question
Statement I: Crime rates in the neighborhood fell. Statement II: Police patrol frequency in the area was increased. Relation:

### Options
- (A) II is cause, I is effect
- (B) I is cause, II is effect
- (C) Both are independent
- (D) Cannot determine

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Cause and Effect, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "II is cause, I is effect" is the valid solution.
