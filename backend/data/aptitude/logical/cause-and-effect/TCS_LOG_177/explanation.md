# Question TCS_LOG_177 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (I is cause, II is effect)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Cause and Effect

### Question
Statement I: Many professionals are opting for remote work. Statement II: Office space rental rates in cities have fallen. Relation:

### Options
- (A) I is cause, II is effect
- (B) II is cause, I is effect
- (C) Independent events
- (D) Both are effects of pandemic

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Cause and Effect, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "I is cause, II is effect" is the valid solution.
