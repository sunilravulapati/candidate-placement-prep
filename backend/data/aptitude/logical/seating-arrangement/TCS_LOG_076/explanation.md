# Question TCS_LOG_076 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (D)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Seating Arrangement

### Question
5 people A,B,C,D,E sit in a row. A is 2nd from left, D is next to A. Who is 3rd if B is at extreme right and C is next to B?

### Options
- (A) D
- (B) C
- (C) E
- (D) A

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Seating Arrangement, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "D" is the valid solution.
