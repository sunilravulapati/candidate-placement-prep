# Question TCS_LOG_079 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (3)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Seating Arrangement

### Question
In a row of 10, X is 4th from left and Y is 7th from right. How many people are between X and Y?

### Options
- (A) 2
- (B) 3
- (C) 1
- (D) 4

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Seating Arrangement, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "3" is the valid solution.
