# Question TCS_LOG_078 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (4th from left)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Seating Arrangement

### Question
P,Q,R,S sit in a row. P is not next to Q. R is next to S. If Q is 2nd from left, where is P?

### Options
- (A) 4th from left
- (B) 1st from left
- (C) 3rd from left
- (D) 2nd from right

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Seating Arrangement, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "4th from left" is the valid solution.
