# Question TCS_LOG_081 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (C or D)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Seating Arrangement

### Question
6 students sit in a row facing north. A is at one end. F is between D and E. B is to the immediate right of A. C is between B and D. Who is in the middle?

### Options
- (A) C or D
- (B) B or C
- (C) D or E
- (D) A or B

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Seating Arrangement, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "C or D" is the valid solution.
