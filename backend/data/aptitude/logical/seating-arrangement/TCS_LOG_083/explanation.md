# Question TCS_LOG_083 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (B)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Seating Arrangement

### Question
A,B,C,D,E sit in a row. B is to the right of E but left of C. A is at extreme left. D is not next to A. Middle position is:

### Options
- (A) B
- (B) C
- (C) E
- (D) D

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Seating Arrangement, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "B" is the valid solution.
