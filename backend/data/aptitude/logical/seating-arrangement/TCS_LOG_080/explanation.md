# Question TCS_LOG_080 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (C)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Seating Arrangement

### Question
A is to the left of B. C is to the right of D. B and D are at the same position. Who is 2nd from right: A,B,C,D?

### Options
- (A) C
- (B) B
- (C) A
- (D) D

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Seating Arrangement, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "C" is the valid solution.
