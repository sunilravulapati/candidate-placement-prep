# Question TCS_LOG_085 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (5)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Seating Arrangement

### Question
In a row of 20, Mohan is 8th from left and Sohan is 14th from left. How many people are between them?

### Options
- (A) 5
- (B) 6
- (C) 4
- (D) 7

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Seating Arrangement, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "5" is the valid solution.
