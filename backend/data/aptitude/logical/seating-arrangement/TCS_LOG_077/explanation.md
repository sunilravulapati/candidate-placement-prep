# Question TCS_LOG_077 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (E)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Seating Arrangement

### Question
6 people sit in a circle. A is between B and C. D is between E and F. B is opposite D. Who is opposite A?

### Options
- (A) E
- (B) F
- (C) C
- (D) B

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Seating Arrangement, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "E" is the valid solution.
