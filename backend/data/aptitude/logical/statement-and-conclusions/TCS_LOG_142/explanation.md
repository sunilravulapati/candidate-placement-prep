# Question TCS_LOG_142 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (The conclusion does not necessarily follow)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Statement and Conclusions

### Question
Statement: The government has implemented a new tax to fund education. Conclusion: Education quality will improve immediately.

### Options
- (A) The conclusion does not necessarily follow
- (B) The conclusion follows
- (C) More information needed
- (D) Statement is incorrect

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Statement and Conclusions, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "The conclusion does not necessarily follow" is the valid solution.
