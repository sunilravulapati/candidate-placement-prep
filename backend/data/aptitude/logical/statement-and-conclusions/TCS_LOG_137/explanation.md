# Question TCS_LOG_137 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Conclusion follows (logically))**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Statement and Conclusions

### Question
Statement: All birds can fly. Penguins are birds. Conclusion: Penguins can fly.

### Options
- (A) Conclusion follows (logically)
- (B) Conclusion does not follow (factually)
- (C) Cannot be determined
- (D) Both A and B

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Statement and Conclusions, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Conclusion follows (logically)" is the valid solution.
