# Question TCS_LOG_138 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Conclusion follows)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Statement and Conclusions

### Question
Statement: No politician is honest. Ram is a politician. Conclusion: Ram is not honest.

### Options
- (A) Conclusion follows
- (B) Conclusion does not follow
- (C) Partially follows
- (D) Cannot say

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Statement and Conclusions, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Conclusion follows" is the valid solution.
