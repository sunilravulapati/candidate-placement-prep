# Question TCS_LOG_143 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Conclusion likely follows)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Statement and Conclusions

### Question
Statement: Regular exercise reduces stress. Meena exercises daily. Conclusion: Meena has reduced stress.

### Options
- (A) Conclusion likely follows
- (B) Conclusion definitely follows
- (C) Conclusion does not follow
- (D) Cannot say

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Statement and Conclusions, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Conclusion likely follows" is the valid solution.
