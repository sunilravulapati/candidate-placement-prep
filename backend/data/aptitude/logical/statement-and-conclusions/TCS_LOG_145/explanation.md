# Question TCS_LOG_145 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Conclusion may partially follow)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Statement and Conclusions

### Question
Statement: Healthy eating prevents diseases. Poor countries have high disease rates. Conclusion: People in poor countries do not eat healthy.

### Options
- (A) Conclusion may partially follow
- (B) Conclusion definitely follows
- (C) Conclusion does not follow
- (D) Needs more data

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Statement and Conclusions, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Conclusion may partially follow" is the valid solution.
