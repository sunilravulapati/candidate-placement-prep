# Question TCS_LOG_141 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Only I follows)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Statement and Conclusions

### Question
Statement: Some cats are dogs. All dogs are black. Conclusion I: Some cats are black. Conclusion II: All black are dogs.

### Options
- (A) Only I follows
- (B) Both follow
- (C) Only II follows
- (D) Neither follows

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Statement and Conclusions, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Only I follows" is the valid solution.
