# Question TCS_LOG_144 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Conclusion does not follow)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Statement and Conclusions

### Question
Statement: Most criminals have a troubled childhood. John had a troubled childhood. Conclusion: John will become a criminal.

### Options
- (A) Conclusion does not follow
- (B) Conclusion follows
- (C) Partially follows
- (D) Cannot determine

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Statement and Conclusions, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Conclusion does not follow" is the valid solution.
