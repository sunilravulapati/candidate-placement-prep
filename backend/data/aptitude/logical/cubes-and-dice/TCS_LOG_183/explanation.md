# Question TCS_LOG_183 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (4)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Cubes and Dice

### Question
On a standard dice, opposite faces add up to 7. If 3 is on top and 4 faces you, which number is at the bottom?

### Options
- (A) 4
- (B) 3
- (C) 5
- (D) 6

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Cubes and Dice, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "4" is the valid solution.
