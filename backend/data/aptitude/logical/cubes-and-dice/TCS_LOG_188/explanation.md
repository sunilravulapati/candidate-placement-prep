# Question TCS_LOG_188 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (64)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Cubes and Dice

### Question
A cube is painted on all faces and cut into 216 smaller cubes. How many have no paint?

### Options
- (A) 64
- (B) 125
- (C) 100
- (D) 150

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Cubes and Dice, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "64" is the valid solution.
