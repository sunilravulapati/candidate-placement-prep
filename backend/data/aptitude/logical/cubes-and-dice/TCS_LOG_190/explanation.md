# Question TCS_LOG_190 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (12)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Cubes and Dice

### Question
A 3×3×3 cube is painted on all faces and cut into 27 unit cubes. How many have paint on exactly 2 faces?

### Options
- (A) 12
- (B) 6
- (C) 8
- (D) 24

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Cubes and Dice, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "12" is the valid solution.
