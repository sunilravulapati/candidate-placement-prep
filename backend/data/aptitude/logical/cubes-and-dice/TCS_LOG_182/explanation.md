# Question TCS_LOG_182 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (6)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Cubes and Dice

### Question
A cube is painted and cut into 27 smaller cubes. How many have exactly 1 painted face?

### Options
- (A) 6
- (B) 12
- (C) 8
- (D) 24

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Cubes and Dice, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "6" is the valid solution.
