# Question TCS_LOG_187 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (24)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Cubes and Dice

### Question
A rectangular block 2×3×4 is cut into 1×1×1 unit cubes. How many unit cubes are there?

### Options
- (A) 24
- (B) 12
- (C) 36
- (D) 16

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Cubes and Dice, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "24" is the valid solution.
