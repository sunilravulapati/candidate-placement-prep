# Question TCS_LOG_185 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (5)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Cubes and Dice

### Question
A die shows 2 on top and 3 facing you. What is at the bottom?

### Options
- (A) 5
- (B) 4
- (C) 6
- (D) 1

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Cubes and Dice, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "5" is the valid solution.
