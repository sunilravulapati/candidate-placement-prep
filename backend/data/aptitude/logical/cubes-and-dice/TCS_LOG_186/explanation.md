# Question TCS_LOG_186 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (8)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Cubes and Dice

### Question
A cube is painted red on all sides and cut into 8 equal smaller cubes. How many small cubes have exactly 3 painted faces?

### Options
- (A) 8
- (B) 4
- (C) 6
- (D) 0

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Cubes and Dice, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "8" is the valid solution.
