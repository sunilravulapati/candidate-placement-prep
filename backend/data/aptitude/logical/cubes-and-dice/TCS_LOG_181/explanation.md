# Question TCS_LOG_181 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (8)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Cubes and Dice

### Question
A cube is painted red on all faces and cut into 64 smaller cubes. How many small cubes have no painted face?

### Options
- (A) 8
- (B) 16
- (C) 24
- (D) 32

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Cubes and Dice, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "8" is the valid solution.
