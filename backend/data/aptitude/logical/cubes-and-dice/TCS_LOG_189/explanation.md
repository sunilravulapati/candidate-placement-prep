# Question TCS_LOG_189 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (3)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Cubes and Dice

### Question
Opposite faces of a die: 1-6, 2-5, 3-4. Die placed with 1 on top, 2 facing front. What is on right?

### Options
- (A) 3
- (B) 4
- (C) 5
- (D) 6

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Cubes and Dice, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "3" is the valid solution.
