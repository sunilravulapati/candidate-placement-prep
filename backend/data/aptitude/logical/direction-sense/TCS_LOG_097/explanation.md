# Question TCS_LOG_097 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (20 m)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Direction Sense

### Question
P walks 10m North, Q starts at same point and walks 10m South. How far apart are they?

### Options
- (A) 20 m
- (B) 10 m
- (C) 0 m
- (D) 15 m

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Direction Sense, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "20 m" is the valid solution.
