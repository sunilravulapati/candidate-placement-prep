# Question TCS_LOG_098 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (At home)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Direction Sense

### Question
From home I walk 5m East, 5m North, 5m West, and 5m South. Where am I?

### Options
- (A) At home
- (B) 5m North
- (C) 5m East
- (D) 5m South

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Direction Sense, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "At home" is the valid solution.
