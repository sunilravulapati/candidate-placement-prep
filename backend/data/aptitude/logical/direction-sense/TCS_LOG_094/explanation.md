# Question TCS_LOG_094 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (At start)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Direction Sense

### Question
A walks 20m South, turns left 10m, turns left 20m, turns left 10m. He is now:

### Options
- (A) At start
- (B) 20m North
- (C) 10m South
- (D) 20m East

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Direction Sense, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "At start" is the valid solution.
