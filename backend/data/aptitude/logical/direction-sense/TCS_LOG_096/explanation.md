# Question TCS_LOG_096 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (5 km)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Direction Sense

### Question
A town map: School is 3km East of Library. Hospital is 4km North of School. How far is Hospital from Library?

### Options
- (A) 5 km
- (B) 7 km
- (C) 4 km
- (D) 6 km

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Direction Sense, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "5 km" is the valid solution.
