# Question TCS_LOG_092 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (At starting point)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Direction Sense

### Question
A walks 3km North, 4km East, 3km South, and 4km West. He is now:

### Options
- (A) At starting point
- (B) 4km East
- (C) 4km West
- (D) 3km North

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Direction Sense, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "At starting point" is the valid solution.
