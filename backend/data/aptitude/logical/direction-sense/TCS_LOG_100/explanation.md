# Question TCS_LOG_100 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (13m (approx))**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Direction Sense

### Question
Ravi walks 12m North, 5m East, and turns South. He walks 3m. How far is he from start?

### Options
- (A) 13m (approx)
- (B) 14m
- (C) 12m
- (D) 15m

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Direction Sense, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "13m (approx)" is the valid solution.
