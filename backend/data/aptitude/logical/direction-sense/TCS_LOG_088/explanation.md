# Question TCS_LOG_088 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (10 km)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Direction Sense

### Question
A goes 6 km East, then 8 km South. How far from starting point?

### Options
- (A) 10 km
- (B) 14 km
- (C) 12 km
- (D) 8 km

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Direction Sense, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "10 km" is the valid solution.
