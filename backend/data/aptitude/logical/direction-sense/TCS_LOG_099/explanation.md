# Question TCS_LOG_099 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Southeast)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Direction Sense

### Question
A man faces West. He turns 45° clockwise, then 180° anti-clockwise. Where does he face?

### Options
- (A) Southeast
- (B) Northwest
- (C) Northeast
- (D) Southwest

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Direction Sense, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Southeast" is the valid solution.
