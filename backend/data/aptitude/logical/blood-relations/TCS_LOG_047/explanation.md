# Question TCS_LOG_047 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Aunt)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Blood Relations

### Question
Pointing to a woman, a man says 'She is the sister of the father of my son.' How is the woman related to the man?

### Options
- (A) Aunt
- (B) Sister
- (C) Mother
- (D) Cousin

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Blood Relations, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Aunt" is the valid solution.
