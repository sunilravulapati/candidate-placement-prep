# Question TCS_LOG_055 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Maternal uncle)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Blood Relations

### Question
Rahul's mother is the only daughter of Monika's father. How is Monika's husband related to Rahul?

### Options
- (A) Maternal uncle
- (B) Father
- (C) Grandfather
- (D) Uncle

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Blood Relations, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Maternal uncle" is the valid solution.
