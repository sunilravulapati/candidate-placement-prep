# Question TCS_LOG_060 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Uncle)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Blood Relations

### Question
A boy says 'That man is the husband of my mother's sister.' The man is the boy's ___

### Options
- (A) Uncle
- (B) Father
- (C) Cousin
- (D) Brother

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Blood Relations, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Uncle" is the valid solution.
