# Question TCS_LOG_051 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Sister)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Blood Relations

### Question
A man said to a woman, 'Your mother's husband's sister is my aunt.' How is the woman related to the man?

### Options
- (A) Sister
- (B) Aunt
- (C) Niece
- (D) Cousin

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Blood Relations, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Sister" is the valid solution.
