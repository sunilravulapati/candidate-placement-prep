# Question TCS_LOG_054 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Niece)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Blood Relations

### Question
X is Y's brother. Y is Z's sister. Z is W's father. W is V's mother. V is X's ___.

### Options
- (A) Niece
- (B) Nephew
- (C) Daughter
- (D) Cousin

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Blood Relations, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Niece" is the valid solution.
