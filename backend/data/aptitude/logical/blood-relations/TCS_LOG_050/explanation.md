# Question TCS_LOG_050 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Daughter)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Blood Relations

### Question
Introducing a girl, a boy says 'She is the daughter of my mother's only son.' How is the girl related to the boy?

### Options
- (A) Daughter
- (B) Niece
- (C) Sister
- (D) Cousin

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Blood Relations, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Daughter" is the valid solution.
