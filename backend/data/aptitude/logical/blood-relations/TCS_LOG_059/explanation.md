# Question TCS_LOG_059 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Son-in-law)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Blood Relations

### Question
P is Q's father. Q is R's sister. S is R's mother. T is S's father. P is T's ___

### Options
- (A) Son-in-law
- (B) Nephew
- (C) Son
- (D) Cousin

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Blood Relations, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Son-in-law" is the valid solution.
