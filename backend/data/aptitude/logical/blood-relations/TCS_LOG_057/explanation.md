# Question TCS_LOG_057 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Cousin)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Blood Relations

### Question
A woman introduces a man as the son of the brother of her mother. How is the man related to the woman?

### Options
- (A) Cousin
- (B) Uncle
- (C) Nephew
- (D) Brother

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Blood Relations, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Cousin" is the valid solution.
