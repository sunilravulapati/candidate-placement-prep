# Question TCS_LOG_053 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Father-in-law)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Blood Relations

### Question
A woman pointing to a photo says 'This man's son is my son's father.' Who is the man in the photo to the woman?

### Options
- (A) Father-in-law
- (B) Father
- (C) Uncle
- (D) Husband

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Blood Relations, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Father-in-law" is the valid solution.
