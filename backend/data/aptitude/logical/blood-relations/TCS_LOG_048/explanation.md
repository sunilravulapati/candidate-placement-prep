# Question TCS_LOG_048 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Grandfather)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Blood Relations

### Question
A + B means A is mother of B; A − B means A is brother of B; A × B means A is father of B; A ÷ B means A is sister of B. If P × Q + R, then P is R's?

### Options
- (A) Grandfather
- (B) Father
- (C) Grandmother
- (D) Maternal uncle

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Blood Relations, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Grandfather" is the valid solution.
