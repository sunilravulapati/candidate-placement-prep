# Question TCS_LOG_169 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option D (He lights a candle)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Puzzles

### Question
Without electricity, how could a man living alone read at night?

### Options
- (A) He is blind and reads Braille
- (B) He uses a phone
- (C) He reads by moonlight
- (D) He lights a candle

### Explanation
Option (D) is the correct answer. In Puzzles, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "He lights a candle" is the valid solution.
