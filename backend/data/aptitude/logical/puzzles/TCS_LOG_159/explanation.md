- (C) Fill 5L and remove 1L

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Puzzles, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Fill 5L, pour into 3L, empty 3L, pour remainder (2L) from 5L into 3L, fill 5L, pour 1L into 3L, leaving 4L in 5L." is the valid solution.
