# Question TCS_LOG_168 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (They are grandfather, father, son (3 people))**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Puzzles

### Question
Two fathers and two sons go fishing. They catch 3 fish total, one each. How?

### Options
- (A) They are grandfather, father, son (3 people)
- (B) Two people
- (C) Four people
- (D) Three fathers

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Puzzles, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "They are grandfather, father, son (3 people)" is the valid solution.
