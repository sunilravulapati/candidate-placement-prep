# Question TCS_LOG_161 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Take chicken across first)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Puzzles

### Question
A river crossing: farmer, fox, chicken, grain. Fox eats chicken; chicken eats grain. Farmer can take one at a time. What is the first move?

### Options
- (A) Take chicken across first
- (B) Take fox across first
- (C) Take grain across first
- (D) Cannot be solved

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Puzzles, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Take chicken across first" is the valid solution.
