# Question TCS_LOG_165 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (57.6 s)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Puzzles

### Question
A train enters a 600m tunnel at 50 km/h. The train is 200m long. How long to pass completely?

### Options
- (A) 57.6 s
- (B) 60 s
- (C) 64 s
- (D) 48 s

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Puzzles, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "57.6 s" is the valid solution.
