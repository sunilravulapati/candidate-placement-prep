# Question TCS_LOG_158 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (20 paise and 10 paise)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Puzzles

### Question
A man has two coins totaling 30 paise. One is not a 10 paise coin. What are the coins?

### Options
- (A) 20 paise and 10 paise
- (B) 25 paise and 5 paise
- (C) 15 paise and 15 paise
- (D) 20 paise and 20 paise

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Puzzles, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "20 paise and 10 paise" is the valid solution.
