# Question TCS_LOG_395 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Some roses may fade quickly)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Logical

### Question
Logical: All roses are flowers. Some flowers fade quickly. Therefore?

### Options
- (A) Some roses may fade quickly
- (B) All roses fade
- (C) No rose fades
- (D) Cannot say

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Logical, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Some roses may fade quickly" is the valid solution.
