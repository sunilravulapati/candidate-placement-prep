# Question TCS_LOG_511 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Brother)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Logical

### Question
Blood rel: A has two children B and C. D is B's daughter. E is D's uncle. How is E related to C?

### Options
- (A) Brother
- (B) Son
- (C) Father
- (D) Nephew

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Logical, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Brother" is the valid solution.
