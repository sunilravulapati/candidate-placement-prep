# Question TCS_LOG_361 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (3km North of start)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Logical

### Question
Direction: Walk 4 km West, turn 90° left, walk 3 km. Then 90° right, walk 4 km East. Where?

### Options
- (A) 3km North of start
- (B) 3km South
- (C) Same place
- (D) 6km West

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Logical, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "3km North of start" is the valid solution.
