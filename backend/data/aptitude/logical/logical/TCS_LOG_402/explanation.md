# Question TCS_LOG_402 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (E or F)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Logical

### Question
6 people in circle. A opposite B. C between A and D. Who is between B and D?

### Options
- (A) E or F
- (B) C
- (C) A
- (D) D

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Logical, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "E or F" is the valid solution.
