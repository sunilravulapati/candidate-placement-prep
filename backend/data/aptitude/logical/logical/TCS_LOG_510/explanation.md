# Question TCS_LOG_510 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Due North)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Logical

### Question
Direction: A is 4m E of B. C is 3m N of A. D is 5m W of C. D from B?

### Options
- (A) Due North
- (B) Due West
- (C) NW direction
- (D) NE direction

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Logical, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Due North" is the valid solution.
