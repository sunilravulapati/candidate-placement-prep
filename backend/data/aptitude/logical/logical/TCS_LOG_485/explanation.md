# Question TCS_LOG_485 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Some cats are small)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Logical

### Question
Syllogism: Some cats are white. All white things are small. Conclusion?

### Options
- (A) Some cats are small
- (B) All cats are small
- (C) No cat is small
- (D) Cannot say

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Logical, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Some cats are small" is the valid solution.
