# Question TCS_LOG_348 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Maternal aunt)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Logical

### Question
Blood relation: If A+B means A is mother of B, A-B means A is brother of B, A*B means A is sister of B, then P+Q-R*S means P is __ of S?

### Options
- (A) Maternal aunt
- (B) Mother
- (C) Uncle
- (D) Niece

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Logical, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Maternal aunt" is the valid solution.
