# Question TCS_LOG_447 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (P is grandfather of R)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Logical

### Question
Blood rel: If A+B = A is husband of B, A-B = A is father of B. P+Q-R means?

### Options
- (A) P is grandfather of R
- (B) P is father of R
- (C) Q is grandfather of R
- (D) R is son of P

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Logical, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "P is grandfather of R" is the valid solution.
