# Question TCS_LOG_507 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Between Q and R)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Logical

### Question
Seating: P Q R S T in circle (equal spaces). P opposite Q. R is right of Q. S is right of P. T is?

### Options
- (A) Between Q and R
- (B) Between S and R
- (C) Between P and S
- (D) Opposite T

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Logical, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Between Q and R" is the valid solution.
