# Question TCS_LOG_618 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Son or Daughter)**

### Topic

### Question

### Options

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Logical, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Son or Daughter" is the valid solution.
