# Question TCS_LOG_408 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Grandfather)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Logical

### Question
Blood relation: If A*B means A is father of B, A/B means A is mother, A+B means A is brother. P*Q/R+S means P is __ of S?

### Options
- (A) Grandfather
- (B) Father
- (C) Grandmother
- (D) Uncle

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Logical, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Grandfather" is the valid solution.
