# Question TCS_LOG_350 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (12)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Logical

### Question
Spatial reasoning: A cube painted red on all faces and cut into 27 equal cubes. How many have paint on exactly 2 faces?

### Options
- (A) 12
- (B) 8
- (C) 6
- (D) 24

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Logical, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "12" is the valid solution.
