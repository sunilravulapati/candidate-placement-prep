# Question TCS_LOG_304 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (X)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Logical

### Question
Seating: 5 people sit in circle. X sits left of Y and right of Z. P is right of Y. Who is between Z and P?

### Options
- (A) X
- (B) Y
- (C) Q
- (D) W

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Logical, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "X" is the valid solution.
