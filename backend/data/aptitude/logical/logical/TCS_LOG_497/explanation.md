# Question TCS_LOG_497 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Grandfather)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Logical

### Question
Blood rel: M is N's father. N is O's daughter. P is O's son. How is M related to P?

### Options
- (A) Grandfather
- (B) Father
- (C) Uncle
- (D) Cousin

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Logical, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Grandfather" is the valid solution.
