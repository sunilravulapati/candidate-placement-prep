# Question TCS_LOG_367 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (4)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Logical

### Question
Seating: 8 people in circle. A sits 3 left of B and 5 right of B. People between them on other side?

### Options
- (A) 4
- (B) 3
- (C) 2
- (D) 5

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Logical, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "4" is the valid solution.
