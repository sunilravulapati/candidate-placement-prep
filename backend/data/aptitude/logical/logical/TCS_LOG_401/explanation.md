# Question TCS_LOG_401 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (In-law)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Logical

### Question
If A is the mother of B and C, C is married to D. D is the son of E. How is E related to A?

### Options
- (A) In-law
- (B) Brother
- (C) Uncle
- (D) Son

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Logical, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "In-law" is the valid solution.
