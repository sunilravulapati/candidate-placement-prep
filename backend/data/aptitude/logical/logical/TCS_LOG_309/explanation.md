# Question TCS_LOG_309 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (E)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Logical

### Question
Puzzle: 5 friends A,B,C,D,E have different weights. D>C>B. A>E. C>A. Who is lightest?

### Options
- (A) E
- (B) B
- (C) A
- (D) D

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Logical, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "E" is the valid solution.
