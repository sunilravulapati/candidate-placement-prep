# Question TCS_LOG_358 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Sister)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Logical

### Question
Blood relation: Pointing to man, woman says he is son of only son of my grandfather. How is she related?

### Options
- (A) Sister
- (B) Cousin
- (C) Daughter
- (D) Aunt

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Logical, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Sister" is the valid solution.
