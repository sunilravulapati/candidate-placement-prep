# Question TCS_LOG_487 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (3 and 7)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Logical

### Question
Age problem: Difference 4 years. In 5 years, older is twice younger. Ages?

### Options
- (A) 3 and 7
- (B) 4 and 8
- (C) 2 and 6
- (D) 5 and 9

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Logical, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "3 and 7" is the valid solution.
