# Question TCS_LOG_311 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Q)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Logical

### Question
Puzzle: P earns more than Q. R earns less than P. S earns more than P. Who earns least?

### Options
- (A) Q
- (B) P
- (C) R
- (D) S

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Logical, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Q" is the valid solution.
