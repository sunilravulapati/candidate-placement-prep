# Question TCS_LOG_194 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Both together are sufficient)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Data Sufficiency

### Question
What is the average age of A, B, C? (I) Average of A and B is 25. (II) C's age is 28.

### Options
- (A) Both together are sufficient
- (B) Statement I alone
- (C) Statement II alone
- (D) Neither alone nor together

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Data Sufficiency, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Both together are sufficient" is the valid solution.
