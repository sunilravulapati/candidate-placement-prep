# Question TCS_LOG_193 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Either I or II alone is sufficient)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Data Sufficiency

### Question
Is triangle ABC equilateral? (I) All sides are equal. (II) All angles are 60°.

### Options
- (A) Either I or II alone is sufficient
- (B) Only I is sufficient
- (C) Only II is sufficient
- (D) Both together are needed

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Data Sufficiency, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Either I or II alone is sufficient" is the valid solution.
