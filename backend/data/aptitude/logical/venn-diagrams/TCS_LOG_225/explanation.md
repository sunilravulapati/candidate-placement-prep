# Question TCS_LOG_225 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (30)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Venn Diagrams

### Question
In a group of 200 students: 100 study Physics, 120 study Chemistry, 50 study both. How many study neither?

### Options
- (A) 30
- (B) 50
- (C) 20
- (D) 40

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Venn Diagrams, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "30" is the valid solution.
