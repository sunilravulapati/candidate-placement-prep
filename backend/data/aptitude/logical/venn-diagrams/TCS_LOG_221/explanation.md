# Question TCS_LOG_221 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (10)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Venn Diagrams

### Question
In a class of 50: 30 like Cricket, 20 like Football, 10 like both. How many like neither?

### Options
- (A) 10
- (B) 15
- (C) 20
- (D) 5

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Venn Diagrams, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "10" is the valid solution.
