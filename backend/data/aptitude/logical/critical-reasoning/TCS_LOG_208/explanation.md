# Question TCS_LOG_208 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Some A may or may not be C)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Critical Reasoning

### Question
All A are B. Some B are C. Therefore:

### Options
- (A) Some A may or may not be C
- (B) All A are C
- (C) No A are C
- (D) Some A are definitely C

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Critical Reasoning, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Some A may or may not be C" is the valid solution.
