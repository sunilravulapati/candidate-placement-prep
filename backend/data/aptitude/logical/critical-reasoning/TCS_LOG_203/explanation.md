# Question TCS_LOG_203 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Ram may or may not have perfect attendance)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Critical Reasoning

### Question
A company policy states: 'Employees with perfect attendance get a bonus.' Ram received a bonus. What can we conclude?

### Options
- (A) Ram may or may not have perfect attendance
- (B) Ram has perfect attendance
- (C) Ram has partial attendance
- (D) Cannot conclude anything

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Critical Reasoning, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Ram may or may not have perfect attendance" is the valid solution.
