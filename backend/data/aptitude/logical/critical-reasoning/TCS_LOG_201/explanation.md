# Question TCS_LOG_201 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (Cities are identical in all other respects)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Critical Reasoning

### Question
Argument: 'The crime rate in cities with CCTV cameras is lower than in cities without. Therefore, CCTV cameras reduce crime.' The assumption is:

### Options
- (A) CCTV cameras are the only difference between the two groups of cities
- (B) Cities are identical in all other respects
- (C) CCTV cameras record all crime
- (D) Police in CCTV cities work harder

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Critical Reasoning, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Cities are identical in all other respects" is the valid solution.
