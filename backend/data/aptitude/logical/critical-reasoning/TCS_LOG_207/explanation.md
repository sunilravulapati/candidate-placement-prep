# Question TCS_LOG_207 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (High-performing students are more likely to own laptops)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Critical Reasoning

### Question
Which weakens the argument: 'Students who use laptops in class perform better on exams.'?

### Options
- (A) High-performing students are more likely to own laptops
- (B) Laptops distract students
- (C) The study covered 5000 students
- (D) Laptops allow better note-taking

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Critical Reasoning, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "High-performing students are more likely to own laptops" is the valid solution.
