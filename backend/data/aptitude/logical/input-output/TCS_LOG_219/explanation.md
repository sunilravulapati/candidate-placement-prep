# Question TCS_LOG_219 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (3 1 4 1 5 9)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Input-Output

### Question
A sorting machine arranges numbers: largest moves to end. After pass 1 on [3,1,4,1,5,9]: result is:

### Options
- (A) 3 1 4 1 5 9
- (B) 1 3 4 1 5 9
- (C) 3 1 4 1 5 9
- (D) 3 1 1 4 5 9

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Input-Output, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "3 1 4 1 5 9" is the valid solution.
