# Question TCS_LOG_212 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (9 8 5 2 3 1)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Input-Output

### Question
In a step process, numbers are arranged in descending order. Input: 5 8 2 9 3 1. Step 2 output is:

### Options
- (A) 9 8 5 2 3 1
- (B) 9 8 2 5 3 1
- (C) 8 9 5 2 3 1
- (D) 9 5 8 2 3 1

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Input-Output, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "9 8 5 2 3 1" is the valid solution.
