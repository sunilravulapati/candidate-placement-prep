# Question TCS_LOG_147 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Both I and II are implicit)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Statement and Assumptions

### Question
Statement: 'Please keep off the grass.' Assumption I: People walk on grass. Assumption II: The grass is fragile.

### Options
- (A) Both I and II are implicit
- (B) Only I is implicit
- (C) Only II is implicit
- (D) Neither is implicit

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Statement and Assumptions, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Both I and II are implicit" is the valid solution.
