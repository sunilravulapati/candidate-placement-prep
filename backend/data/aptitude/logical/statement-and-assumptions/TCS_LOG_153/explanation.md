# Question TCS_LOG_153 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Both are implicit)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Statement and Assumptions

### Question
Statement: 'If you are interested in the post, apply before 30th November.' Assumption I: Some people are interested in the post. Assumption II: Applications after 30th November will be rejected.

### Options
- (A) Both are implicit
- (B) Only I is implicit
- (C) Only II is implicit
- (D) Neither is implicit

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Statement and Assumptions, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Both are implicit" is the valid solution.
