# Question TCS_LOG_155 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Only I is implicit)**

### Topic
Logical Reasoning > Statement and Assumptions

### Question
Statement: 'Books are better companions than television.' Assumption I: Books are educational. Assumption II: Television is not educational.

### Options
- (A) Only I is implicit
- (B) Only II is implicit
- (C) Both are implicit
- (D) Neither is implicit

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Statement and Assumptions, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Only I is implicit" is the valid solution.
