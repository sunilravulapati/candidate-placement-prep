# Question TCS_APT_180 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (36)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > HCF and LCM

### Question
The HCF of two numbers is 12 and their LCM is 144. If one number is 48, find the other.

### Options
- (A) 36
- (B) 24
- (C) 48
- (D) 60

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In HCF and LCM, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "36" is the valid solution.
