# Question TCS_APT_182 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (180)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > HCF and LCM

### Question
The least number exactly divisible by 12, 15, 18 is:

### Options
- (A) 180
- (B) 120
- (C) 90
- (D) 150

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In HCF and LCM, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "180" is the valid solution.
