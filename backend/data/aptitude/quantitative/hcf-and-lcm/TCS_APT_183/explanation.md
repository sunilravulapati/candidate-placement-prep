# Question TCS_APT_183 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (10:12 AM)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > HCF and LCM

### Question
Two bells ring every 18 and 24 minutes. If they ring together at 9 AM, when will they next ring together?

### Options
- (A) 10:12 AM
- (B) 9:42 AM
- (C) 10:00 AM
- (D) 9:48 AM

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In HCF and LCM, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "10:12 AM" is the valid solution.
