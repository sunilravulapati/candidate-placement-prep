# Question TCS_APT_179 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (12m)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > HCF and LCM

### Question
Three ropes of lengths 36m, 48m, and 60m are to be cut into equal pieces. Maximum piece length?

### Options
- (A) 12m
- (B) 6m
- (C) 18m
- (D) 24m

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In HCF and LCM, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "12m" is the valid solution.
