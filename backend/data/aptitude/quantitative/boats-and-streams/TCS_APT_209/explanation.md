# Question TCS_APT_209 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (9 km/h)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Boats and Streams

### Question
A boat speed is 12 km/h, stream speed is 3 km/h. Speed upstream?

### Options
- (A) 9 km/h
- (B) 15 km/h
- (C) 10 km/h
- (D) 8 km/h

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Boats and Streams, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "9 km/h" is the valid solution.
