# Question TCS_APT_211 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (8 km/h)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Boats and Streams

### Question
A boat goes 30 km upstream in 5h and 30 km downstream in 3h. Find boat speed in still water.

### Options
- (A) 8 km/h
- (B) 7 km/h
- (C) 6 km/h
- (D) 10 km/h

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Boats and Streams, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "8 km/h" is the valid solution.
