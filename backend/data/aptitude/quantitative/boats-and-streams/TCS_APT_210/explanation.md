# Question TCS_APT_210 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (1.5 km/h)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Boats and Streams

### Question
A man rows 18 km downstream in 2h and 12 km upstream in 3h. Speed of current?

### Options
- (A) 1.5 km/h
- (B) 2 km/h
- (C) 1 km/h
- (D) 3 km/h

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Boats and Streams, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "1.5 km/h" is the valid solution.
