# Question TCS_APT_212 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (4 hours)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Boats and Streams

### Question
Speed of boat in still water is 10 km/h and stream speed is 2 km/h. Time to cover 48 km downstream?

### Options
- (A) 4 hours
- (B) 5 hours
- (C) 6 hours
- (D) 3 hours

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Boats and Streams, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "4 hours" is the valid solution.
