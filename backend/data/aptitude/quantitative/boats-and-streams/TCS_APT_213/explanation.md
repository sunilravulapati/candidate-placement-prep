# Question TCS_APT_213 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (3.75 hours)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Boats and Streams

### Question
A person can row at 8 km/h in still water. River flows at 2 km/h. Time to row 15 km downstream and return?

### Options
- (A) 4.5 hours
- (B) 3.75 hours
- (C) 4 hours
- (D) 5 hours

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Boats and Streams, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "3.75 hours" is the valid solution.
