# Question TCS_APT_442 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (16/3 days)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Time and Work

### Question
A woman can do work in 16 days. A man does twice as fast. Together in a team of 1 man 1 woman, days needed:

### Options
- (A) 16/3 days
- (B) 6 days
- (C) 4 days
- (D) 8 days

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Time and Work, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "16/3 days" is the valid solution.
