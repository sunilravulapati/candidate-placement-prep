# Question TCS_APT_440 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (10)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Time and Work

### Question
If 18 pumps can raise 2170 tonnes of water in 10 days working 7 hours a day, how many pumps needed to raise 1736 tonnes in 8 days working 9 hours?

### Options
- (A) 10
- (B) 12
- (C) 14
- (D) 8

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Time and Work, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "10" is the valid solution.
