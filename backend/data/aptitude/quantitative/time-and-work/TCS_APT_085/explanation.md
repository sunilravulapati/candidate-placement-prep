# Question TCS_APT_085 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (30 days)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Time and Work

### Question
A and B together can do a piece of work in 12 days. B alone takes 20 days. How many days does A alone take?

### Options
- (A) 30 days
- (B) 24 days
- (C) 25 days
- (D) 28 days

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Time and Work, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "30 days" is the valid solution.
