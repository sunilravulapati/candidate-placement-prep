# Question TCS_APT_441 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (3.5 hrs)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Time and Work

### Question
A taps fills tank in 6 hrs, B in 4 hrs. Both opened, after 1 hr A closed. Total time to fill?

### Options
- (A) 3.5 hrs
- (B) 2.5 hrs
- (C) 4 hrs
- (D) 3 hrs

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Time and Work, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "3.5 hrs" is the valid solution.
