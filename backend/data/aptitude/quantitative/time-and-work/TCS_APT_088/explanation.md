# Question TCS_APT_088 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (4.8 hours)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Time and Work

### Question
Pipe A fills in 6 hours, Pipe B fills in 8 hours. Both open and leakage drains in 24 hours. Time to fill:

### Options
- (A) 4.8 hours
- (B) 5 hours
- (C) 6 hours
- (D) 4 hours

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Time and Work, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "4.8 hours" is the valid solution.
