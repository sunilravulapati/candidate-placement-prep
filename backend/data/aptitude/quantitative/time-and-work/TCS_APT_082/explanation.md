# Question TCS_APT_082 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (16 days)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Time and Work

### Question
20 men can complete a job in 30 days. After 10 days, 5 more men join. How many more days are needed?

### Options
- (A) 16 days
- (B) 18 days
- (C) 20 days
- (D) 15 days

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Time and Work, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "16 days" is the valid solution.
