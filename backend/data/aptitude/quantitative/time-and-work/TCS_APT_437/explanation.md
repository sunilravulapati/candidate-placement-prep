# Question TCS_APT_437 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (12 days)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Time and Work

### Question
A and B can do a work in 6 and 12 days. With help of C they finish in 4 days. C alone?

### Options
- (A) 12 days
- (B) 8 days
- (C) 16 days
- (D) 10 days

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Time and Work, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "12 days" is the valid solution.
