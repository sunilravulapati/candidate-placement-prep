# Question TCS_APT_086 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (4 days)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Time and Work

### Question
Three workers A, B, C finish a job in 10, 12, and 15 days respectively. Working together, they complete the job in:

### Options
- (A) 4 days
- (B) 5 days
- (C) 3 days
- (D) 6 days

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Time and Work, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "4 days" is the valid solution.
