# Question TCS_APT_084 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (20 days)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Time and Work

### Question
A can complete 40% of a task in 8 days. At the same rate, how many days to complete the whole task?

### Options
- (A) 20 days
- (B) 15 days
- (C) 25 days
- (D) 18 days

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Time and Work, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "20 days" is the valid solution.
