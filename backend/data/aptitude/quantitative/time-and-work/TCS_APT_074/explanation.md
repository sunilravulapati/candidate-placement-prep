# Question TCS_APT_074 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (6 days)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Time and Work

### Question
A can do a piece of work in 10 days and B in 15 days. How long will they take working together?

### Options
- (A) 6 days
- (B) 5 days
- (C) 7 days
- (D) 8 days

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Time and Work, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "6 days" is the valid solution.
