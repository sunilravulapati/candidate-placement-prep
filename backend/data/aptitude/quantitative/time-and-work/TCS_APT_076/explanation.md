# Question TCS_APT_076 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (24 days)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Time and Work

### Question
A and B together complete a work in 8 days. A alone does it in 12 days. In how many days can B alone do it?

### Options
- (A) 24 days
- (B) 18 days
- (C) 20 days
- (D) 30 days

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Time and Work, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "24 days" is the valid solution.
