# Question TCS_APT_439 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (24 days)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Time and Work

### Question
40 workers can build a wall in 18 days. 10 workers leave after 6 days. How many more days needed?

### Options
- (A) 24 days
- (B) 20 days
- (C) 22 days
- (D) 18 days

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Time and Work, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "24 days" is the valid solution.
