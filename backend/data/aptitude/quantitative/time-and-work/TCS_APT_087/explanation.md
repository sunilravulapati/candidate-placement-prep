# Question TCS_APT_087 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (30 days)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Time and Work

### Question
A does half as much work as B in 3/4 of the time. If together they take 18 days, how long does B take alone?

### Options
- (A) 30 days
- (B) 24 days
- (C) 40 days
- (D) 36 days

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Time and Work, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "30 days" is the valid solution.
