# Question TCS_APT_075 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (10)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Time and Work

### Question
A can do a job in 12 days and B in 18 days. If A works alone for 4 days, how many more days will B need to finish?

### Options
- (A) 10
- (B) 12
- (C) 8
- (D) 14

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Time and Work, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "10" is the valid solution.
