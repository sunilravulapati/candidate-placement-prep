# Question TCS_APT_081 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (200 minutes)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Time and Work

### Question
Pipe A fills a tank in 40 minutes and Pipe B empties it in 50 minutes. If both are open simultaneously, when will the tank be full?

### Options
- (A) 200 minutes
- (B) 180 minutes
- (C) 160 minutes
- (D) 240 minutes

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Time and Work, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "200 minutes" is the valid solution.
