# Question TCS_APT_080 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (16/3 days)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Time and Work

### Question
A and B can do a work in 6 days, B and C in 8 days, A and C in 12 days. In how many days can all three do it together?

### Options
- (A) 16/3 days
- (B) 6 days
- (C) 5 days
- (D) 8 days

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Time and Work, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "16/3 days" is the valid solution.
