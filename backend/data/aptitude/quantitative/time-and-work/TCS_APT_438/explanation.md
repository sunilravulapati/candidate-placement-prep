# Question TCS_APT_438 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (13.33 days)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Time and Work

### Question
A does 1/4 of work in 5 days. B does 1/2 of remaining work in 10 days. Together they finish in:

### Options
- (A) 13.33 days
- (B) 15 days
- (C) 12 days
- (D) 18 days

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Time and Work, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "13.33 days" is the valid solution.
