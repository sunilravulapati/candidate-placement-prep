# Question TCS_APT_461 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (n(n+1)/2)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Algebra

### Question
Sum of first n natural numbers formula is:

### Options
- (A) n(n+1)/2
- (B) n(n-1)/2
- (C) n2
- (D) n(n+2)/2

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Algebra, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "n(n+1)/2" is the valid solution.
