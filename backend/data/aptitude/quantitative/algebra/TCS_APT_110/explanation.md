# Question TCS_APT_110 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (2 and 3)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Algebra

### Question
If x2 - 5x + 6 = 0, the values of x are:

### Options
- (A) 2 and 3
- (B) 1 and 6
- (C) −2 and −3
- (D) 2 and −3

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Algebra, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "2 and 3" is the valid solution.
