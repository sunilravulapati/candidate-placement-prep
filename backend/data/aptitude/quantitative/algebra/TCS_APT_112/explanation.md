# Question TCS_APT_112 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (2)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Algebra

### Question
If 2x − 3y = 4 and 3x + y = 7, find x.

### Options
- (A) 1.8
- (B) 2
- (C) 1.5
- (D) 2.5

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Algebra, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "2" is the valid solution.
