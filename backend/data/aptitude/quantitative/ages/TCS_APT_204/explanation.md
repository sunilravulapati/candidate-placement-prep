# Question TCS_APT_204 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (10)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Ages

### Question
Father is 4 times son's age. In 20 years, father will be twice son's age. Find son's current age.

### Options
- (A) 10
- (B) 15
- (C) 12
- (D) 8

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Ages, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "10" is the valid solution.
