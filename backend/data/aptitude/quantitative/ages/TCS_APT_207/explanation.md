# Question TCS_APT_207 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (10)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Ages

### Question
A mother is 30 years older than her daughter. In 5 years, mother will be 3 times daughter's age. Find daughter's current age.

### Options
- (A) 10
- (B) 8
- (C) 12
- (D) 15

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Ages, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "10" is the valid solution.
