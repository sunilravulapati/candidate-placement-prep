# Question TCS_APT_162 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (16)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Permutation and Combination

### Question
A coin is tossed 5 times. How many outcomes have at least 3 heads?

### Options
- (A) 16
- (B) 15
- (C) 20
- (D) 12

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Permutation and Combination, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "16" is the valid solution.
