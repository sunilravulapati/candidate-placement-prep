# Question TCS_APT_157 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (210)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Permutation and Combination

### Question
A committee of 4 is to be chosen from 7 men and 5 women. How many committees have exactly 2 women?

### Options
- (A) 210
- (B) 120
- (C) 90
- (D) 180

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Permutation and Combination, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "210" is the valid solution.
