# Question TCS_APT_495 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (48)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Permutation and Combination

### Question
How many 4-digit even numbers can be formed using 1,2,3,4,5 (no repetition)?

### Options
- (A) 48
- (B) 60
- (C) 36
- (D) 72

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Permutation and Combination, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "48" is the valid solution.
