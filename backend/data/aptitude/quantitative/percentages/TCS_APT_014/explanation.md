# Question TCS_APT_014 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Rs.391)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Percentages

### Question
A product costs Rs.400. Its price is increased by 15% and then decreased by 15%. What is the final price?

### Options
- (A) Rs.391
- (B) Rs.400
- (C) Rs.385
- (D) Rs.395

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Percentages, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Rs.391" is the valid solution.
