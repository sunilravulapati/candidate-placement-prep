# Question TCS_APT_413 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (19%)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Percentages

### Question
If the radius of a circle is reduced by 10%, its area is reduced by:

### Options
- (A) 19%
- (B) 10%
- (C) 20%
- (D) 18%

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Percentages, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "19%" is the valid solution.
