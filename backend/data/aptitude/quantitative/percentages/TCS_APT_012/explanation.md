# Question TCS_APT_012 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (6.25% decrease)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Percentages

### Question
A number is first increased by 25% and then decreased by 25%. The net change is:

### Options
- (A) 6.25% decrease
- (B) No change
- (C) 6.25% increase
- (D) 0.25% increase

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Percentages, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "6.25% decrease" is the valid solution.
