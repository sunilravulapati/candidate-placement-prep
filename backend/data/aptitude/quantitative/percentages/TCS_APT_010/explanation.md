# Question TCS_APT_010 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (16.67%)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Percentages

### Question
If A is 20% more than B, then B is what percent less than A?

### Options
- (A) 16.67%
- (B) 20%
- (C) 18%
- (D) 25%

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Percentages, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "16.67%" is the valid solution.
