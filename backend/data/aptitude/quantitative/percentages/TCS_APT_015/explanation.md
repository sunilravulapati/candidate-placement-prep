# Question TCS_APT_015 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (24000)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Percentages

### Question
Three candidates contest an election. One gets 45% votes and another gets 35%. The third candidate gets 2400 votes. Find the total votes.

### Options
- (A) 24000
- (B) 20000
- (C) 22000
- (D) 25000

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Percentages, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "24000" is the valid solution.
