# Question TCS_APT_008 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (10%)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Percentages

### Question
If the population of a town increased from 50000 to 55000, what is the percentage increase?

### Options
- (A) 10%
- (B) 5%
- (C) 15%
- (D) 8%

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Percentages, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "10%" is the valid solution.
