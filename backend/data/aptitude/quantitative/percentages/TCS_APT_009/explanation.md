# Question TCS_APT_009 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (12.5%)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Percentages

### Question
A shopkeeper marks his goods 25% above the cost price and allows a 10% discount. What is his profit percentage?

### Options
- (A) 12.5%
- (B) 15%
- (C) 10%
- (D) 12%

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Percentages, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "12.5%" is the valid solution.
