# Question TCS_APT_003 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (4% decrease)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Percentages

### Question
A number is increased by 20% and then decreased by 20%. What is the net change?

### Options
- (A) 4% decrease
- (B) No change
- (C) 4% increase
- (D) 2% decrease

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Percentages, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "4% decrease" is the valid solution.
