# Question TCS_APT_011 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (5%)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Percentages

### Question
In an examination, 70% of students passed in English and 65% in Maths. If 40% passed in both, what percentage failed in both?

### Options
- (A) 5%
- (B) 10%
- (C) 15%
- (D) 25%

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Percentages, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "5%" is the valid solution.
