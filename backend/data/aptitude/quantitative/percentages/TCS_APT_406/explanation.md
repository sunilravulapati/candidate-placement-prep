# Question TCS_APT_406 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (25%)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Percentages

### Question
A's salary is 20% less than B's. By what percent is B's salary more than A's?

### Options
- (A) 25%
- (B) 20%
- (C) 22%
- (D) 15%

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Percentages, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "25%" is the valid solution.
