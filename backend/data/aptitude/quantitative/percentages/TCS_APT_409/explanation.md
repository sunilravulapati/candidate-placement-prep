# Question TCS_APT_409 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (80%)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Percentages

### Question
A trader mixes 80L pure milk with 20L water. What % of the mixture is milk?

### Options
- (A) 80%
- (B) 75%
- (C) 85%
- (D) 90%

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Percentages, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "80%" is the valid solution.
