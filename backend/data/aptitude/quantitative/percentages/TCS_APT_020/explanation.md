# Question TCS_APT_020 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (60%)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Percentages

### Question
In a class of 100 students, 40 are girls. What percentage are boys?

### Options
- (A) 60%
- (B) 40%
- (C) 50%
- (D) 70%

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Percentages, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "60%" is the valid solution.
