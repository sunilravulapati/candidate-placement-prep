# Question TCS_APT_005 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (1% decrease)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Percentages

### Question
A price is first increased by 10% and then decreased by 10%. The net effect is:

### Options
- (A) 1% decrease
- (B) No change
- (C) 1% increase
- (D) 2% decrease

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Percentages, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "1% decrease" is the valid solution.
