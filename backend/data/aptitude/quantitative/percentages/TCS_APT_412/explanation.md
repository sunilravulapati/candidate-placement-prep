# Question TCS_APT_412 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (37.5%)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Percentages

### Question
A man spends 75% of his income. If his income increases by 20% and he still spends the same amount, what % of new income does he save?

### Options
- (A) 37.5%
- (B) 30%
- (C) 25%
- (D) 40%

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Percentages, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "37.5%" is the valid solution.
