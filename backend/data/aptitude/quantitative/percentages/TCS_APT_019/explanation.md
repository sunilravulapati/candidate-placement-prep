# Question TCS_APT_019 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (20%)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Percentages

### Question
If A's income is 25% more than B's, by what percent is B's income less than A's?

### Options
- (A) 20%
- (B) 25%
- (C) 22%
- (D) 18%

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Percentages, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "20%" is the valid solution.
