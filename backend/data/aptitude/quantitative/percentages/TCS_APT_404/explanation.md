# Question TCS_APT_404 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (14.5%)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Percentages

### Question
A shopkeeper offers 2 successive discounts of 10% and 5%. What is the effective discount?

### Options
- (A) 14.5%
- (B) 15%
- (C) 13.5%
- (D) 12%

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Percentages, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "14.5%" is the valid solution.
