# Question TCS_APT_428 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Rs.10000)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Compound Interest

### Question
If the difference between SI and CI for 2 years at 10% is Rs.100, the principal is:

### Options
- (A) Rs.10000
- (B) Rs.8000
- (C) Rs.12000
- (D) Rs.9000

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Compound Interest, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Rs.10000" is the valid solution.
