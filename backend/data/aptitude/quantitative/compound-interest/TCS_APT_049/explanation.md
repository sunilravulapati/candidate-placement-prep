# Question TCS_APT_049 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Rs.210)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Compound Interest

### Question
Compound interest on Rs.1000 at 10% p.a. for 2 years (compounded annually) is:

### Options
- (A) Rs.210
- (B) Rs.200
- (C) Rs.250
- (D) Rs.220

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Compound Interest, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Rs.210" is the valid solution.
