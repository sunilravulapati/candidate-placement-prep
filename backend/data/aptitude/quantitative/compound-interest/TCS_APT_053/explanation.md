# Question TCS_APT_053 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Rs.2662)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Compound Interest

### Question
Find the amount on Rs.2000 at 10% p.a. compounded annually for 3 years.

### Options
- (A) Rs.2662
- (B) Rs.2600
- (C) Rs.2700
- (D) Rs.2640

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Compound Interest, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Rs.2662" is the valid solution.
