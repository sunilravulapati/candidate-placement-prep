# Question TCS_APT_054 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Rs.8)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Compound Interest

### Question
The difference between CI and SI on Rs.5000 for 2 years at 4% is:

### Options
- (A) Rs.8
- (B) Rs.10
- (C) Rs.12
- (D) Rs.6

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Compound Interest, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Rs.8" is the valid solution.
