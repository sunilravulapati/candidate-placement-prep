# Question TCS_APT_052 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Rs.1000)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Compound Interest

### Question
A sum becomes Rs.1331 in 3 years at 10% compounded annually. Find the sum.

### Options
- (A) Rs.1000
- (B) Rs.1100
- (C) Rs.1200
- (D) Rs.900

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Compound Interest, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Rs.1000" is the valid solution.
