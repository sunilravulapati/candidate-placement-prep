# Question TCS_APT_058 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (4.2 years)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Compound Interest

### Question

### Options
- (A) 4.2 years
- (B) 5 years

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Compound Interest, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "4.2 years" is the valid solution.
