# Question TCS_APT_171 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (5/8)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Probability

### Question
A box contains 3 white and 5 black balls. Probability of NOT drawing a white ball?

### Options
- (A) 5/8
- (B) 3/8
- (C) 1/2
- (D) 2/5

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Probability, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "5/8" is the valid solution.
