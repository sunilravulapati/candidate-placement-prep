# Question TCS_APT_165 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (1/2)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Probability

### Question

### Options

- (B) 1/4
- (C) 3/4
- (D) 2/4

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Probability, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "1/2" is the valid solution.
