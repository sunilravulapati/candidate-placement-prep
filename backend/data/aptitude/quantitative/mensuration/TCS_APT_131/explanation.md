# Question TCS_APT_131 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (54 m2)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Mensuration

### Question
A room 5m × 4m × 3m. Area of 4 walls is:

### Options
- (A) 54 m2
- (B) 60 m2
- (C) 48 m2
- (D) 70 m2

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Mensuration, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "54 m2" is the valid solution.
