# Question TCS_APT_507 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (11:14)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Mensuration

### Question
If the perimeter of a square equals the circumference of a circle (π=22/7), ratio of areas (square:circle)?

### Options
- (A) 11:14
- (B) 22:7
- (C) 7:11
- (D) 14:11

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Mensuration, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "11:14" is the valid solution.
