# Question TCS_APT_504 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (720°)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Mensuration

### Question
The sum of interior angles of a hexagon is:

### Options
- (A) 720°
- (B) 540°
- (C) 630°
- (D) 810°

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Mensuration, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "720°" is the valid solution.
