# Question TCS_APT_503 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (12)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Mensuration

### Question

### Options

- (B) 10

- (D) 8

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Mensuration, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "12" is the valid solution.
