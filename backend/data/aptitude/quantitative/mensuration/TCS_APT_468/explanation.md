# Question TCS_APT_468 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (4 times)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Mensuration

### Question
A cylinder has same radius and height. If radius doubles, volume becomes:

### Options
- (A) 4 times
- (B) 2 times
- (C) 8 times
- (D) 6 times

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Mensuration, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "4 times" is the valid solution.
