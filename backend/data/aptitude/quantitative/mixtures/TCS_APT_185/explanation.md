# Question TCS_APT_185 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (20 L)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Mixtures

### Question
A 40L mixture has 30% alcohol. How much water must be added to make alcohol 20%?

### Options
- (A) 20 L
- (B) 25 L
- (C) 15 L
- (D) 10 L

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Mixtures, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "20 L" is the valid solution.
