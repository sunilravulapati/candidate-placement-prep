# Question TCS_APT_188 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Rs.11.52/kg)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Mixtures

### Question
Two varieties of rice, Rs.8/kg and Rs.12/kg, are mixed in ratio 3:2. Selling price for 20% profit:

### Options
- (A) Rs.11.52/kg
- (B) Rs.12/kg
- (C) Rs.10.5/kg
- (D) Rs.13/kg

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Mixtures, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Rs.11.52/kg" is the valid solution.
