# Question TCS_APT_187 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (38.4 L)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Mixtures

### Question
A vessel contains 60L of juice. 12L is drawn and replaced with water, then 12L again drawn and replaced. Final juice amount:

### Options
- (A) 38.4 L
- (B) 40 L
- (C) 36 L
- (D) 42 L

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Mixtures, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "38.4 L" is the valid solution.
