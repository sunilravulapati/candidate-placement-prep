# Question TCS_APT_184 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (5 L)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Mixtures

### Question
A mixture of milk and water in ratio 3:1. To make ratio 3:2, how much water should be added to 20L?

### Options
- (A) 4 L
- (B) 5 L
- (C) 6 L
- (D) 3 L

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Mixtures, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "5 L" is the valid solution.
