# Question TCS_APT_186 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (2:1)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Mixtures

### Question
By rule of alligation, in what ratio must 5% and 20% acid solutions be mixed to get 10% solution?

### Options
- (A) 2:1
- (B) 1:2
- (C) 3:1
- (D) 1:1

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Mixtures, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "2:1" is the valid solution.
