# Question TCS_APT_202 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (24 hours)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Pipes and Cisterns

### Question
A cistern has two inlets and one outlet. Inlets fill in 8 and 12 hours, outlet drains in 6 hours. Time to fill when all open:

### Options
- (A) 24 hours
- (B) 12 hours
- (C) 18 hours
- (D) 16 hours

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Pipes and Cisterns, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "24 hours" is the valid solution.
