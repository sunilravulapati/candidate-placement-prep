# Question TCS_APT_201 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (4/3 hours)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Pipes and Cisterns

### Question
3 pipes can fill a tank in 3, 4, and 6 hours. Opened together, tank fills in:

### Options
- (A) 4/3 hours
- (B) 2 hours
- (C) 1.5 hours
- (D) 3 hours

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Pipes and Cisterns, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "4/3 hours" is the valid solution.
