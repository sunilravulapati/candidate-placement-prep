# Question TCS_APT_199 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (60/7 min)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Pipes and Cisterns

### Question
Pipe A fills a tank in 15 min and Pipe B in 20 min. Both open together fill tank in:

### Options
- (A) 60/7 min
- (B) 8 min
- (C) 10 min
- (D) 12 min

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Pipes and Cisterns, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "60/7 min" is the valid solution.
