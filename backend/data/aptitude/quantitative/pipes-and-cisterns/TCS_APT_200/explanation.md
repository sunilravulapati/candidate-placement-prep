# Question TCS_APT_200 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (90 min)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Pipes and Cisterns

### Question
A pipe fills a tank in 30 min. A leak empties it in 45 min. With both, tank is full in:

### Options
- (A) 90 min
- (B) 120 min
- (C) 60 min
- (D) 75 min

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Pipes and Cisterns, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "90 min" is the valid solution.
