# Question TCS_APT_203 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (6 hours)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Pipes and Cisterns

### Question
Pipe A fills 1/3 of tank in 5 hours. Pipe B fills rest in 10 hours. Together they fill in:

### Options
- (A) 6 hours
- (B) 8 hours
- (C) 5 hours
- (D) 10 hours

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Pipes and Cisterns, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "6 hours" is the valid solution.
