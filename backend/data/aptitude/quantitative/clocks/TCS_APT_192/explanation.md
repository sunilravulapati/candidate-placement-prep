# Question TCS_APT_192 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (12 hours)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Clocks

### Question
A clock gains 5 minutes every hour. In how many hours will it show 1 hour ahead?

### Options
- (A) 12 hours
- (B) 10 hours
- (C) 15 hours
- (D) 8 hours

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Clocks, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "12 hours" is the valid solution.
