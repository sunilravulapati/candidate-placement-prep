# Question TCS_APT_189 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (130°)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Clocks

### Question
At 3:40, the angle between clock hands is:

### Options
- (A) 130°
- (B) 140°
- (C) 120°
- (D) 150°

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Clocks, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "130°" is the valid solution.
