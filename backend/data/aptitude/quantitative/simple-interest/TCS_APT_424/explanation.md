# Question TCS_APT_424 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Rs.3000)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Simple Interest

### Question
A sum becomes Rs.3600 in 2 years and Rs.4200 in 4 years at SI. The principal is:

### Options
- (A) Rs.3000
- (B) Rs.2800
- (C) Rs.3200
- (D) Rs.2500

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Simple Interest, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Rs.3000" is the valid solution.
