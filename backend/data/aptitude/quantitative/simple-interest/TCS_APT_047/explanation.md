# Question TCS_APT_047 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (5%)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Simple Interest

### Question
A sum of Rs.800 amounts to Rs.920 in 3 years at simple interest. What is the rate?

### Options
- (A) 5%
- (B) 4%
- (C) 6%
- (D) 3%

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Simple Interest, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "5%" is the valid solution.
