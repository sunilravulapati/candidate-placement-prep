# Question TCS_APT_045 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Rs.6000)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Simple Interest

### Question
The SI on a certain sum for 2 years at 12% p.a. is Rs.1440. Find the sum.

### Options
- (A) Rs.6000
- (B) Rs.5000
- (C) Rs.7000
- (D) Rs.4800

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Simple Interest, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Rs.6000" is the valid solution.
