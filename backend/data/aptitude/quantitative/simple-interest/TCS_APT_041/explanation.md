# Question TCS_APT_041 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Rs.300)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Simple Interest

### Question
Simple interest on Rs.2000 at 5% per annum for 3 years is:

### Options
- (A) Rs.300
- (B) Rs.400
- (C) Rs.200
- (D) Rs.350

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Simple Interest, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Rs.300" is the valid solution.
