# Question TCS_APT_048 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (3 years)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Simple Interest

### Question
In what time will Rs.3000 yield Rs.900 as interest at 10% per annum?

### Options
- (A) 3 years
- (B) 4 years
- (C) 2.5 years
- (D) 5 years

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Simple Interest, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "3 years" is the valid solution.
