# Question TCS_APT_044 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (10)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Simple Interest

### Question

### Options

- (B) 8
- (C) 12

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Simple Interest, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "10" is the valid solution.
