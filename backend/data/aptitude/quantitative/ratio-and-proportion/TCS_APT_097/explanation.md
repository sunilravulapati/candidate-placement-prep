# Question TCS_APT_097 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Rs.8000)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Ratio and Proportion

### Question
Salaries of A, B, C are in ratio 1:2:3. If salary of C is Rs.12000, what is the average salary?

### Options
- (A) Rs.8000
- (B) Rs.6000
- (C) Rs.10000
- (D) Rs.4000

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Ratio and Proportion, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Rs.8000" is the valid solution.
