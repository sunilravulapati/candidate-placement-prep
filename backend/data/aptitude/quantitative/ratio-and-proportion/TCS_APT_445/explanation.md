# Question TCS_APT_445 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (45 and 60)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Ratio and Proportion

### Question
Two numbers are in ratio 3:4. Their LCM is 180. The numbers are:

### Options
- (A) 45 and 60
- (B) 36 and 48
- (C) 60 and 80
- (D) 54 and 72

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Ratio and Proportion, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "45 and 60" is the valid solution.
