# Question TCS_APT_098 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (5 and 8)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Ratio and Proportion

### Question
If 4 is subtracted from each term of ratio 5:8, ratio becomes 1:2. Find original numbers.

### Options
- (A) 5 and 8
- (B) 10 and 16
- (C) 15 and 24
- (D) 4 and 8

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Ratio and Proportion, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "5 and 8" is the valid solution.
