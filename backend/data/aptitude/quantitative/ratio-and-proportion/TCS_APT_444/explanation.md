# Question TCS_APT_444 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Rs.2400)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Ratio and Proportion

### Question
Rs.5400 is divided among A, B, C in ratio 1/2 : 1/3 : 1/4. A's share?

### Options
- (A) Rs.2400
- (B) Rs.1800
- (C) Rs.1200
- (D) Rs.2000

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Ratio and Proportion, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Rs.2400" is the valid solution.
