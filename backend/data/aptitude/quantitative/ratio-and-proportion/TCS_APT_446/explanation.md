# Question TCS_APT_446 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (40 L)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Ratio and Proportion

### Question
Milk and water in ratio 5:3. If 16L of mixture removed and 16L water added, ratio becomes 3:5. Original quantity?

### Options
- (A) 40 L
- (B) 32 L
- (C) 48 L
- (D) 50 L

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Ratio and Proportion, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "40 L" is the valid solution.
