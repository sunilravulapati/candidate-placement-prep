# Question TCS_APT_096 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (70 litres)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Ratio and Proportion

### Question
A mixture contains milk and water in ratio 7:3. If 10 litres of water is added, ratio becomes 7:4. Find initial milk.

### Options
- (A) 70 litres
- (B) 60 litres
- (C) 80 litres
- (D) 50 litres

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Ratio and Proportion, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "70 litres" is the valid solution.
