# Question TCS_APT_092 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (24)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Ratio and Proportion

### Question
The ages of A and B are in ratio 4:5. Six years hence the ratio will be 5:6. Find A's age.

### Options
- (A) 24
- (B) 20
- (C) 28
- (D) 30

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Ratio and Proportion, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "24" is the valid solution.
