# Question TCS_APT_093 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (48)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Ratio and Proportion

### Question
A bag has coins in ratio 2:3:5 for Re.1, 50p, and 25p. Total Rs.114. How many 50p coins?

### Options
- (A) 48
- (B) 60
- (C) 36
- (D) 72

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Ratio and Proportion, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "48" is the valid solution.
