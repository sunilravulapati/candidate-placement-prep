# Question TCS_APT_089 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (5:8)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Ratio and Proportion

### Question
If A:B = 3:4 and B:C = 5:6, what is A:C?

### Options
- (A) 5:8
- (B) 15:24
- (C) 3:6
- (D) 5:6

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Ratio and Proportion, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "5:8" is the valid solution.
