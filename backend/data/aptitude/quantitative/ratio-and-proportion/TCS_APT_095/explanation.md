# Question TCS_APT_095 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (12 and 20)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Ratio and Proportion

### Question
Two numbers are in ratio 3:5. If 4 is added to each, ratio becomes 2:3. Find the numbers.

### Options
- (A) 12 and 20
- (B) 15 and 25
- (C) 9 and 15
- (D) 6 and 10

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Ratio and Proportion, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "12 and 20" is the valid solution.
