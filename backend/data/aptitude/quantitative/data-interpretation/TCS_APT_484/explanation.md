# Question TCS_APT_484 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Rs.3600)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Data Interpretation

### Question
If total expenses in a pie chart are Rs.12000 and food sector is 30%, food expense is:

### Options
- (A) Rs.3600
- (B) Rs.4000
- (C) Rs.3000
- (D) Rs.2400

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Data Interpretation, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Rs.3600" is the valid solution.
