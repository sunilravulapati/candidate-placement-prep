# Question TCS_APT_399 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (12 and 21)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
Two numbers are in ratio 4:7. If 3 is added to each, ratio becomes 3:5. Find the numbers.

### Options
- (A) 12 and 21
- (B) 8 and 14
- (C) 16 and 28
- (D) 4 and 7

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "12 and 21" is the valid solution.
