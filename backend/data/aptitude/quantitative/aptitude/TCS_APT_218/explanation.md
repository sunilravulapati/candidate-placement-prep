# Question TCS_APT_218 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (26%)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
A shopkeeper marks a price 40% above cost and gives 10% discount. What is the profit %?

### Options
- (A) 26%
- (B) 30%
- (C) 24%
- (D) 28%

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "26%" is the valid solution.
