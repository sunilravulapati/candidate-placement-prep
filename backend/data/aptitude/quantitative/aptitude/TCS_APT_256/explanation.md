# Question TCS_APT_256 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (40)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
Father is 4 times older than son. 5 years hence, father will be 3 times son. Find father's age.

### Options
- (A) 40
- (B) 35
- (C) 45
- (D) 50

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "40" is the valid solution.
