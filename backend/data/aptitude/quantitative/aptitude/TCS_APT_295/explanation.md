# Question TCS_APT_295 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (1.5 km/h)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
A man rows 18 km downstream in 2h and returns in 3h. Speed of stream?

### Options
- (A) 1.5 km/h
- (B) 2 km/h
- (C) 1 km/h
- (D) 2.5 km/h

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "1.5 km/h" is the valid solution.
