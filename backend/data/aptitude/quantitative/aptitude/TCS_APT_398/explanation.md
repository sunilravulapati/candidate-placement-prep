# Question TCS_APT_398 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (5%)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
A sum of Rs 2000 earns Rs 400 in simple interest in 4 years. Rate per annum?

### Options
- (A) 5%
- (B) 4%
- (C) 6%
- (D) 8%

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "5%" is the valid solution.
