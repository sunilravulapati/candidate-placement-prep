# Question TCS_APT_263 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (1:1)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
In what ratio must rice at Rs 12/kg be mixed with rice at Rs 16/kg to get mixture at Rs 14/kg?

### Options
- (A) 1:1
- (B) 2:1
- (C) 1:2
- (D) 3:1

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "1:1" is the valid solution.
