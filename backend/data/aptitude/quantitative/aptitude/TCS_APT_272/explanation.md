# Question TCS_APT_272 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (1540 cm3)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
A cylinder has radius 7cm and height 10cm. Find volume (π=22/7).

### Options
- (A) 1540 cm3
- (B) 1000 cm3
- (C) 1200 cm3
- (D) 1320 cm3

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "1540 cm3" is the valid solution.
