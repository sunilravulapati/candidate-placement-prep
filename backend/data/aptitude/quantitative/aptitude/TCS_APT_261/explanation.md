# Question TCS_APT_261 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (59)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
Average weight of 40 students is 55 kg. New student joins, avg becomes 55.1 kg. New student's weight?

### Options
- (A) 59
- (B) 61
- (C) 63
- (D) 55

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "59" is the valid solution.
