# Question TCS_APT_397 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Rs 25)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
If ink costs Rs 5 per bottle and each bottle prints 200 sheets, cost per 1000 sheets of printing?

### Options
- (A) Rs 25
- (B) Rs 20
- (C) Rs 30
- (D) Rs 15

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Rs 25" is the valid solution.
