# Question TCS_APT_241 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (500)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
By selling an article for Rs 560, a man gains 12%. Find cost price.

### Options
- (A) 500
- (B) 480
- (C) 520
- (D) 460

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "500" is the valid solution.
