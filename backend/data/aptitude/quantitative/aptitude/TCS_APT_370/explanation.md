# Question TCS_APT_370 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Rs 20000)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
A salesman's commission is 15% on first Rs 10000 and 10% on rest. If he earns Rs 2500, what were sales?

### Options
- (A) Rs 20000
- (B) Rs 15000
- (C) Rs 25000
- (D) Rs 18000

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Rs 20000" is the valid solution.
