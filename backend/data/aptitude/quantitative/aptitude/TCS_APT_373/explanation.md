# Question TCS_APT_373 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Rs 15)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
A shopkeeper has 100kg sugar. He sells it at 20% profit at Rs 18/kg. His cost price per kg?

### Options
- (A) Rs 15
- (B) Rs 12
- (C) Rs 14
- (D) Rs 16

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Rs 15" is the valid solution.
