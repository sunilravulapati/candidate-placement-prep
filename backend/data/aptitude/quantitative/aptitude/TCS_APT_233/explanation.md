# Question TCS_APT_233 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (6 days)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
Two workers A and B complete a job. A takes 18 days, B takes 9 days. Working together?

### Options
- (A) 6 days
- (B) 5 days
- (C) 4 days
- (D) 7 days

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "6 days" is the valid solution.
