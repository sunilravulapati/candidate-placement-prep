# Question TCS_APT_299 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (20 days)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question

### Options

- (B) 25 days

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "20 days" is the valid solution.
