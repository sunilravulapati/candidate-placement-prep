# Question TCS_APT_328 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (50%)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
In a class 40% play cricket, 30% play football, 20% play both. Play neither?

### Options
- (A) 50%
- (B) 40%
- (C) 30%
- (D) 60%

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "50%" is the valid solution.
