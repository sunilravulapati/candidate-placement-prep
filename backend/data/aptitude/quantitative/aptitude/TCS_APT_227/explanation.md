# Question TCS_APT_227 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (60 min)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
Walking at 4/5 of usual speed, a man is 15 min late. Find usual time.

### Options
- (A) 60 min
- (B) 75 min
- (C) 90 min
- (D) 45 min

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "60 min" is the valid solution.
