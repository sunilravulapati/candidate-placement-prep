# Question TCS_APT_283 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (24 and 18)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question

### Options
- (A) 24 and 18
- (B) 20 and 22

- (D) 25 and 17

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "24 and 18" is the valid solution.
