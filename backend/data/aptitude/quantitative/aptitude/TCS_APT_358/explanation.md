# Question TCS_APT_358 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (36min)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
Two pipes fill tank in 30min and 45min. Third drains in 60min. Time to fill when all open?

### Options
- (A) 36min
- (B) 40min
- (C) 45min
- (D) 30min

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "36min" is the valid solution.
