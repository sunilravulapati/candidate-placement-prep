# Question TCS_APT_254 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (22)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
A man is 24 years older than his son. In 2 years his age will be twice his son's. Find son's age now.

### Options
- (A) 22
- (B) 20
- (C) 24
- (D) 18

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "22" is the valid solution.
