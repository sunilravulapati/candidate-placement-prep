# Question TCS_APT_627 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (7/15)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
A and B alternate, A starts. A can do in 10, B in 15. After 5 days how much done?

### Options
- (A) 7/15
- (B) 1/2
- (C) 8/15
- (D) 5/12

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "7/15" is the valid solution.
