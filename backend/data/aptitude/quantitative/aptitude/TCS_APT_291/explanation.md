# Question TCS_APT_291 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (20000)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
In an election, candidate A gets 55% votes. Winning margin is 2000 votes. Total votes?

### Options
- (A) 20000
- (B) 10000
- (C) 15000
- (D) 25000

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "20000" is the valid solution.
