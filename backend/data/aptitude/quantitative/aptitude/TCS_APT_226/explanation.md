# Question TCS_APT_226 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (50 km/h)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
A car covers 120 km at 40 km/h and 180 km at 60 km/h. Find average speed.

### Options
- (A) 52 km/h
- (B) 50 km/h
- (C) 48 km/h
- (D) 55 km/h

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "50 km/h" is the valid solution.
