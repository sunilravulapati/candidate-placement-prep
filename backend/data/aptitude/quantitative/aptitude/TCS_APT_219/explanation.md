# Question TCS_APT_219 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (25%)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
If the price of an article falls by 20%, by what % must sales increase to maintain revenue?

### Options
- (A) 25%
- (B) 20%
- (C) 15%
- (D) 30%

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "25%" is the valid solution.
