# Question TCS_APT_255 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (35)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
The sum of ages of A and B is 45 years. 5 years ago, A was twice as old as B. Find A's current age.

### Options
- (A) 35
- (B) 30
- (C) 25
- (D) 40

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "35" is the valid solution.
