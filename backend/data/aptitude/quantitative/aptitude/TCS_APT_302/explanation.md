# Question TCS_APT_302 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (1000)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
A and B invest Rs 3000 and Rs 5000. Profit at end of year is Rs 1600. B's share?

### Options
- (A) 1000
- (B) 800
- (C) 600
- (D) 1200

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "1000" is the valid solution.
