# Question TCS_APT_225 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (6 sec)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
Two trains of 100m and 150m run at 60 km/h and 90 km/h in opposite directions. Time to cross?

### Options
- (A) 6 sec
- (B) 8 sec
- (C) 10 sec
- (D) 5 sec

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "6 sec" is the valid solution.
