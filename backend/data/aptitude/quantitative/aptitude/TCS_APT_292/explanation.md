# Question TCS_APT_292 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (83600)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
Population of a city is 80000. It increases by 10% in yr1 and decreases by 5% in yr2. Final pop?

### Options
- (A) 83600
- (B) 84000
- (C) 86000
- (D) 82000

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "83600" is the valid solution.
