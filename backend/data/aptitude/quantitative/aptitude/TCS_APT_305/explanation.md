# Question TCS_APT_305 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (9:8)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
A and B started business with Rs 5000 each. After 4 months A invested Rs 1000 more. Year-end ratio?

### Options
- (A) 9:8
- (B) 5:4
- (C) 7:6
- (D) 3:2

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "9:8" is the valid solution.
