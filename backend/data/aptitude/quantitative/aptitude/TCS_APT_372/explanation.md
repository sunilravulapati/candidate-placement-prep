# Question TCS_APT_372 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Rs 81000)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
A car's price depreciated by 10% per year. Original price Rs 100000. Value after 2 years?

### Options
- (A) Rs 81000
- (B) Rs 80000
- (C) Rs 90000
- (D) Rs 82000

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Rs 81000" is the valid solution.
