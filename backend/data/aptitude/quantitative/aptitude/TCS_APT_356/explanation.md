# Question TCS_APT_356 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Rs 9)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
If cost of 5 pens and 7 pencils is Rs 87, and 4 pens and 6 pencils is Rs 72, find cost of pen.

### Options
- (A) Rs 9
- (B) Rs 6
- (C) Rs 12
- (D) Rs 8

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Rs 9" is the valid solution.
