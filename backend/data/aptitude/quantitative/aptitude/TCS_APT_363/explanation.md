# Question TCS_APT_363 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (20%)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
The price of a commodity rose by 25%. By what % must consumption decrease to maintain expenditure?

### Options
- (A) 20%
- (B) 25%
- (C) 15%
- (D) 30%

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "20%" is the valid solution.
