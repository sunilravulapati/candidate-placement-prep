# Question TCS_APT_388 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (15)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
Age of A is 3/5 of B. After 5 years, sum of ages = 50. Find A's age now.

### Options
- (A) 15
- (B) 12
- (C) 18
- (D) 20

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "15" is the valid solution.
