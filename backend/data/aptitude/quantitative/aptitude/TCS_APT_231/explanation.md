# Question TCS_APT_231 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (About 7.38 days)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
A and B together finish in 8 days, B and C in 12 days, A and C in 16 days. All three together?

### Options
- (A) About 7.38 days
- (B) 6 days
- (C) 8 days
- (D) 9 days

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "About 7.38 days" is the valid solution.
