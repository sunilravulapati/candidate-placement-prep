# Question TCS_APT_239 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (230)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
The cost price of an article is Rs 200. It is sold at a profit of 15%. Find selling price.

### Options
- (A) 230
- (B) 240
- (C) 220
- (D) 250

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "230" is the valid solution.
