# Question TCS_APT_284 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (24 and 48)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
If the sum of two numbers is 72 and one is twice the other, find the numbers.

### Options
- (A) 24 and 48
- (B) 36 and 36
- (C) 20 and 52
- (D) 30 and 42

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "24 and 48" is the valid solution.
