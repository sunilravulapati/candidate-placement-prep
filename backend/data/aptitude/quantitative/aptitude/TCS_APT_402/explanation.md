# Question TCS_APT_402 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (54)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
A cube of side 5cm is painted on all faces. How many unit cubes have exactly 1 face painted?

### Options
- (A) 54
- (B) 27
- (C) 8
- (D) 18

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "54" is the valid solution.
