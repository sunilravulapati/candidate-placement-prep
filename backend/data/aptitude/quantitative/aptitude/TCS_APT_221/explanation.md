# Question TCS_APT_221 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (60)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
Two numbers are in ratio 3:5. If their sum is 160, find the smaller number.

### Options
- (A) 60
- (B) 80
- (C) 50
- (D) 70

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "60" is the valid solution.
