# Question TCS_APT_240 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (200)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "200" is the valid solution.
