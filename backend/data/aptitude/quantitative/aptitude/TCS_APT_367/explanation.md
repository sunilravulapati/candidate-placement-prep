# Question TCS_APT_367 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (2 km/h)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
A man rows downstream at 12 km/h and upstream at 8 km/h. Speed of current?

### Options
- (A) 2 km/h
- (B) 3 km/h
- (C) 4 km/h
- (D) 1 km/h

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "2 km/h" is the valid solution.
