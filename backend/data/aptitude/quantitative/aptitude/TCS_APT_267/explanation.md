# Question TCS_APT_267 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (12.8L)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
A container has 20L milk. 4L removed and replaced with water. Repeated twice. Milk remaining?

### Options
- (A) 12.8L
- (B) 13.6L
- (C) 11.2L
- (D) 14.4L

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "12.8L" is the valid solution.
