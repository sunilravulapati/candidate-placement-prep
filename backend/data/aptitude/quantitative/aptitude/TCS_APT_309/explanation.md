# Question TCS_APT_309 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (270 km)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
A bus travels at 60 km/h for 2.5h, then 80 km/h for 1.5h. Total distance?

### Options
- (A) 270 km
- (B) 300 km
- (C) 250 km
- (D) 280 km

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "270 km" is the valid solution.
