# Question TCS_APT_360 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (12:15)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
A clock gains 15 min per day. Set correctly at noon Mon. What time on Tue noon does it show?

### Options
- (A) 12:15
- (B) 1:00 PM
- (C) 12:30
- (D) 11:45 AM

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "12:15" is the valid solution.
