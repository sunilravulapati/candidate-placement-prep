# Question TCS_APT_624 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (1/12 per day)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
A can do job in 15 days, does it for 5 days, leaves. B finishes in 8 days. B's rate?

### Options
- (A) 1/12 per day
- (B) 1/10
- (C) 1/8
- (D) 1/15

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "1/12 per day" is the valid solution.
