# Question TCS_APT_242 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (10%)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
A man buys at Rs 750 and sells at Rs 675. Find loss %.

### Options
- (A) 10%
- (B) 8%
- (C) 12%
- (D) 5%

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "10%" is the valid solution.
