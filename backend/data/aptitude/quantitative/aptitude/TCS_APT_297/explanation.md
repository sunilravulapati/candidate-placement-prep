# Question TCS_APT_297 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (16)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question

### Options

- (B) 20

- (D) 24

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "16" is the valid solution.
