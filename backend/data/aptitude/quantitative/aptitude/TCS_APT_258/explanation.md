# Question TCS_APT_258 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (47.5)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question

### Options

- (B) 47.5
- (C) 50
- (D) 42

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "47.5" is the valid solution.
