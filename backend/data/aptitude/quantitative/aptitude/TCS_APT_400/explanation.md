# Question TCS_APT_400 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (8cm and 15cm)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
Perimeter of a right triangle is 40cm with hypotenuse 17cm. Other two sides?

### Options
- (A) 7cm and 16cm
- (B) 8cm and 15cm
- (C) 9cm and 14cm
- (D) 10cm and 13cm

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "8cm and 15cm" is the valid solution.
