# Question TCS_APT_232 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (about 5.5 days)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
A does 1/4 work in 3 days, B does 1/3 work in 4 days. Together how many days to finish?

### Options
- (A) about 5.5 days
- (B) 6 days
- (C) 7 days
- (D) 4.5 days

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "about 5.5 days" is the valid solution.
