# Question TCS_APT_294 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (3h 26min)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
A cistern can be filled by pipes A and B in 6h and 8h. Opened together. Time to fill 3/4 tank?

### Options
- (A) 3h 26min
- (B) 2h 24min
- (C) 4h
- (D) 2h

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "3h 26min" is the valid solution.
