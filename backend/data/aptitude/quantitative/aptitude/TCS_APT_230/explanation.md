# Question TCS_APT_230 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (24 hours)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
A pipe fills a tank in 6 hours. Another drains it in 8 hours. Both open, time to fill?

### Options
- (A) 24 hours
- (B) 20 hours
- (C) 16 hours
- (D) 18 hours

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "24 hours" is the valid solution.
