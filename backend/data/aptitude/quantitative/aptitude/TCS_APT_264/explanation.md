# Question TCS_APT_264 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (30)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
A milkman mixes water and milk in ratio 1:3. If mixture is 40L, how many litres of milk?

### Options
- (A) 30
- (B) 20
- (C) 25
- (D) 35

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "30" is the valid solution.
