# Question TCS_APT_351 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (about 10.9m)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
A 12m ladder leans against wall, foot 5m from wall. How high up wall?

### Options
- (A) about 10.9m
- (B) 11m
- (C) 10m
- (D) 12m

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "about 10.9m" is the valid solution.
