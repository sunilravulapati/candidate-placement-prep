# Question TCS_APT_334 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (5/9)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question

### Options

- (B) 4/9
- (C) 1/2
- (D) 2/3

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "5/9" is the valid solution.
