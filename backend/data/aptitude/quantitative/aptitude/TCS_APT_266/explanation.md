# Question TCS_APT_266 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (11)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question

### Options

- (B) 12
- (C) 10
- (D) 13

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "11" is the valid solution.
