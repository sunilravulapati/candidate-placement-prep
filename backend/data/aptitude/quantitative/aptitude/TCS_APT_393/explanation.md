# Question TCS_APT_393 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (4/3)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
A fraction when added to its reciprocal gives 25/12. The fraction is?

### Options
- (A) 4/3
- (B) 3/4
- (C) 5/4
- (D) 3/5

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "4/3" is the valid solution.
