# Question TCS_APT_570 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (2:1)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
5 men + 10 boys do work in 6 days. 10 men + 5 boys in 4 days. Men vs boys efficiency?

### Options
- (A) 1:2
- (B) 2:1
- (C) 1:1
- (D) 3:1

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "2:1" is the valid solution.
