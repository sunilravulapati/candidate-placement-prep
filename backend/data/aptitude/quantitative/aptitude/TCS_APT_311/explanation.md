# Question TCS_APT_311 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (About 18.2%)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
A man saves Rs 1200 from salary of Rs 6000. Save % next month if salary rises 10% and savings unchanged?

### Options
- (A) About 18.2%
- (B) 20%
- (C) 15%
- (D) 16.7%

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "About 18.2%" is the valid solution.
