# Question TCS_APT_544 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (100m)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
A runs at 10 km/h, B at 15 km/h. 300m race. B gives A head start of how much to tie?

### Options
- (A) 100m
- (B) 50m
- (C) 150m
- (D) 200m

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "100m" is the valid solution.
