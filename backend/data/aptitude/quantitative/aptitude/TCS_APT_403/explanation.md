# Question TCS_APT_403 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (220 cm2)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Aptitude

### Question
A cylinder with radius 7cm and height 5cm. Lateral surface area (π=22/7)?

### Options
- (A) 220 cm2
- (B) 154 cm2
- (C) 440 cm2
- (D) 110 cm2

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Aptitude, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "220 cm2" is the valid solution.
