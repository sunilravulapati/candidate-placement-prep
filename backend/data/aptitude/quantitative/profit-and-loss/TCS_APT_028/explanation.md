# Question TCS_APT_028 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (18)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Profit and Loss

### Question
By selling 24 pens for Rs.120, a man gains 20%. How many pens should be sold for Rs.120 to gain 60%?

### Options
- (A) 18
- (B) 20
- (C) 16
- (D) 14

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Profit and Loss, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "18" is the valid solution.
