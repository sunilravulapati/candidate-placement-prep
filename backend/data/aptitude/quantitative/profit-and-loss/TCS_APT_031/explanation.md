# Question TCS_APT_031 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Rs.176)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Profit and Loss

### Question
By selling an article for Rs.144, a loss of 10% is incurred. To get a profit of 10%, what should be the selling price?

### Options
- (A) Rs.176
- (B) Rs.180
- (C) Rs.170
- (D) Rs.184

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Profit and Loss, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Rs.176" is the valid solution.
