# Question TCS_APT_417 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (11.11%)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Profit and Loss

### Question
A dishonest dealer sells at cost price but uses 900g instead of 1kg. Profit%?

### Options
- (A) 11.11%
- (B) 10%
- (C) 9%
- (D) 12%

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Profit and Loss, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "11.11%" is the valid solution.
