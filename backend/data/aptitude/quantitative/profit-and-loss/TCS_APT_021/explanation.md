# Question TCS_APT_021 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (25%)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Profit and Loss

### Question
A trader buys an article for Rs.400 and sells it for Rs.500. What is his profit percentage?

### Options
- (A) 25%
- (B) 20%
- (C) 30%
- (D) 15%

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Profit and Loss, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "25%" is the valid solution.
