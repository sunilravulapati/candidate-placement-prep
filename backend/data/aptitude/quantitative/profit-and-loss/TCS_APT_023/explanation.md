# Question TCS_APT_023 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Rs.400)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Profit and Loss

### Question
An article is sold at a gain of 15%. If the selling price is Rs.460, find the cost price.

### Options
- (A) Rs.400
- (B) Rs.380
- (C) Rs.420
- (D) Rs.440

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Profit and Loss, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Rs.400" is the valid solution.
