# Question TCS_APT_024 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (10%)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Profit and Loss

### Question
If cost price is Rs.500 and selling price is Rs.450, what is the loss percentage?

### Options
- (A) 10%
- (B) 8%
- (C) 12%
- (D) 15%

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Profit and Loss, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "10%" is the valid solution.
