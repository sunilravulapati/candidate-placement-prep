# Question TCS_APT_036 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Rs.1000)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Profit and Loss

### Question
A chair is sold at a loss of 8% for Rs.920. Find the cost price.

### Options
- (A) Rs.1000
- (B) Rs.1100
- (C) Rs.900
- (D) Rs.1050

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Profit and Loss, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Rs.1000" is the valid solution.
