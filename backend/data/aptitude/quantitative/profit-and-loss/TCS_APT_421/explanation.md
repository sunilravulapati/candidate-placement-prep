# Question TCS_APT_421 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (125%)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Profit and Loss

### Question
After allowing a 12% discount on marked price, a shopkeeper still gains 10%. Marked price is what % of CP?

### Options
- (A) 125%
- (B) 120%
- (C) 130%
- (D) 115%

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Profit and Loss, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "125%" is the valid solution.
