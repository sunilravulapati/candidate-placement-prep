# Question TCS_APT_022 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Rs.800)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Profit and Loss

### Question
A shopkeeper sells an article for Rs.720 at a loss of 10%. What is the cost price?

### Options
- (A) Rs.800
- (B) Rs.900
- (C) Rs.700
- (D) Rs.750

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Profit and Loss, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Rs.800" is the valid solution.
