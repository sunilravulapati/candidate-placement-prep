# Question TCS_APT_029 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (11.1%)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Profit and Loss

### Question
A trader has a false weight that is 10% less than the standard. What is his actual profit percentage if he sells at cost price?

### Options
- (A) 11.1%
- (B) 10%
- (C) 12%
- (D) 9%

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Profit and Loss, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "11.1%" is the valid solution.
