# Question TCS_APT_027 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Rs.240)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Profit and Loss

### Question
If a book is sold at Rs.252 and there is a gain of 5%, what is the cost price?

### Options
- (A) Rs.240
- (B) Rs.200
- (C) Rs.220
- (D) Rs.260

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Profit and Loss, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Rs.240" is the valid solution.
