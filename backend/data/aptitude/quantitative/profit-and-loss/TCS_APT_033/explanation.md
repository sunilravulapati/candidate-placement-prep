# Question TCS_APT_033 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Rs.150)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Profit and Loss

### Question
A retailer purchased 50 kg of rice at Rs.22/kg and sold it at Rs.25/kg. What is the profit?

### Options
- (A) Rs.150
- (B) Rs.200
- (C) Rs.100
- (D) Rs.250

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Profit and Loss, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Rs.150" is the valid solution.
