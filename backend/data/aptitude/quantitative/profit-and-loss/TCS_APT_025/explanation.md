# Question TCS_APT_025 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (1% loss)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Profit and Loss

### Question
A man sells two items each at Rs.990. He gains 10% on one and loses 10% on the other. What is the overall result?

### Options
- (A) 1% loss
- (B) No profit no loss
- (C) 1% gain
- (D) 0.5% loss

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Profit and Loss, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "1% loss" is the valid solution.
