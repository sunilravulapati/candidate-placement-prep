# Question TCS_APT_034 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Rs.20)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Profit and Loss

### Question
A person bought 20 items at Rs.5 each and sold them all at Rs.6 each. What is the total profit?

### Options
- (A) Rs.20
- (B) Rs.15
- (C) Rs.25
- (D) Rs.10

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Profit and Loss, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Rs.20" is the valid solution.
