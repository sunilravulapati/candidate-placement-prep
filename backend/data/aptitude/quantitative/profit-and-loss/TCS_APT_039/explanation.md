# Question TCS_APT_039 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Rs.2000)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Profit and Loss

### Question
A sells a bicycle to B at 20% profit. B sells it to C at 25% profit. If C paid Rs.3000, what did A pay?

### Options
- (A) Rs.2000
- (B) Rs.2400
- (C) Rs.1800
- (D) Rs.2200

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Profit and Loss, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Rs.2000" is the valid solution.
