# Question TCS_APT_419 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option D (6.25% loss)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Profit and Loss

### Question
A man buys two articles for Rs.480 each. He sells one at 25% profit and other at 25% loss. Net result?

### Options
- (A) Neither profit nor loss
- (B) 25% profit
- (C) 25% loss
- (D) 6.25% loss

### Explanation
Option (D) is the correct answer. In Profit and Loss, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "6.25% loss" is the valid solution.
