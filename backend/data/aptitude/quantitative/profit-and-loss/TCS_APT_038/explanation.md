# Question TCS_APT_038 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Rs.750)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Profit and Loss

### Question
A man gains 20% by selling at Rs.720. If he wants a 25% gain, the selling price should be:

### Options
- (A) Rs.750
- (B) Rs.760
- (C) Rs.740
- (D) Rs.780

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Profit and Loss, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Rs.750" is the valid solution.
