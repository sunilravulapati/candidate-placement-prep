# Question TCS_APT_030 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option C (35)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Profit and Loss

### Question
A person sells 36 oranges for Rs.100 and gains 8%. How many oranges did he buy for Rs.100?

### Options
- (A) 38.88
- (B) 40
- (C) 35
- (D) 37

### Explanation
Option (C) is the correct answer. In Profit and Loss, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "35" is the valid solution.
