# Question TCS_APT_198 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option D (Can be 30 or 31)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Calendars

### Question
A month has exactly 5 Sundays. If the month starts on Sunday, the month has:

### Options
- (A) 31 days
- (B) 28 days
- (C) 30 days
- (D) Can be 30 or 31

### Explanation
Option (D) is the correct answer. In Calendars, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Can be 30 or 31" is the valid solution.
