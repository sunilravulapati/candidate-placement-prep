# Question TCS_APT_193 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Monday)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Calendars

### Question
January 1, 2000 was Saturday. What day was January 1, 2001?

### Options
- (A) Monday
- (B) Sunday
- (C) Tuesday
- (D) Wednesday

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Calendars, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Monday" is the valid solution.
