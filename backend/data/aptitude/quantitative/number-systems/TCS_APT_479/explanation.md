# Question TCS_APT_479 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (2^4 × 33)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Number Systems

### Question
What is the LCM of 2^4 × 32 and 22 × 33?

### Options
- (A) 2^4 × 33
- (B) 2^4 × 32
- (C) 22 × 33
- (D) 2^6 × 3^5

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Number Systems, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "2^4 × 33" is the valid solution.
