# Question TCS_APT_470 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (a2-b2)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Number Systems

### Question
What is the HCF of (a2-b2) and (a3-b3)?

### Options
- (A) a2-b2
- (B) a-b
- (C) a3-b3
- (D) a+b

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Number Systems, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "a2-b2" is the valid solution.
