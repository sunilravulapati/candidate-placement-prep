# Question TCS_APT_450 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (48)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Averages

### Question
A cricketer's average in 30 innings is 40. After 10 more innings average is 42. Average in last 10 innings?

### Options
- (A) 48
- (B) 46
- (C) 50
- (D) 44

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Averages, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "48" is the valid solution.
