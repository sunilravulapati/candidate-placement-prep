# Question TCS_APT_448 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (25)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Averages

### Question
Average of 50 numbers is 30. Average of first 25 is 35. Average of last 25 is:

### Options
- (A) 25
- (B) 20
- (C) 30
- (D) 28

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Averages, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "25" is the valid solution.
