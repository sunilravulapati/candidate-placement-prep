# Question TCS_APT_451 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (26 kg)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Averages

### Question
Average weight of class increases by 0.2 kg when a student weighing 30 kg joins. New class has 21 students. Original average?

### Options
- (A) 26 kg
- (B) 30 kg
- (C) 25 kg
- (D) 28 kg

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Averages, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "26 kg" is the valid solution.
