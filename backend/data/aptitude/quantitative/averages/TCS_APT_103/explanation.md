# Question TCS_APT_103 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (60)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Averages

### Question
The average weight of 10 men is 70 kg. One man of 80 kg leaves and another joins. New average is 69 kg. New man's weight:

### Options
- (A) 70
- (B) 60
- (C) 68
- (D) 75

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Averages, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "60" is the valid solution.
