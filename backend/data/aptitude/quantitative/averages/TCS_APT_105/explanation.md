# Question TCS_APT_105 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (36°C)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Averages

### Question
The average temperature of Mon, Tue, Wed was 37°C. For Tue, Wed, Thu it was 38°C. If Thursday's temp was 39°C, what was Monday's?

### Options
- (A) 36°C
- (B) 35°C
- (C) 38°C
- (D) 37°C

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Averages, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "36°C" is the valid solution.
