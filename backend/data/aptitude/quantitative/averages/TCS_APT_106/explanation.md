# Question TCS_APT_106 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (56)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Averages

### Question
A batsman's average after 30 innings is 25. After the 31st innings, average becomes 26. The 31st innings score is:

### Options
- (A) 56
- (B) 55
- (C) 60
- (D) 50

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Averages, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "56" is the valid solution.
