# Question TCS_APT_099 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (38)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Averages

### Question
The average of 5 numbers is 30. If one number is excluded, the average becomes 28. The excluded number is:

### Options
- (A) 38
- (B) 40
- (C) 36
- (D) 32

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Averages, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "38" is the valid solution.
