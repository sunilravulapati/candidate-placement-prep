# Question TCS_APT_517 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (log(b)/log(a))**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Logarithms

### Question
Change of base formula: loga(b) = ?

### Options
- (A) log(b)/log(a)
- (B) log(a)/log(b)
- (C) log(a+b)
- (D) log(ab)

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Logarithms, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "log(b)/log(a)" is the valid solution.
