# Question TCS_APT_065 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (43.6 km/h)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Speed and Distance

### Question
A man covers 4/5 of his journey at 40 km/h and the rest at 60 km/h. What is his average speed?

### Options
- (A) 43.6 km/h
- (B) 45 km/h
- (C) 42 km/h
- (D) 50 km/h

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Speed and Distance, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "43.6 km/h" is the valid solution.
