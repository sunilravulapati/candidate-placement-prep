# Question TCS_APT_069 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (270 km)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Speed and Distance

### Question
Two cars start from the same point in opposite directions at 40 and 50 km/h. After 3 hours, the distance between them is:

### Options
- (A) 270 km
- (B) 200 km
- (C) 250 km
- (D) 300 km

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Speed and Distance, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "270 km" is the valid solution.
