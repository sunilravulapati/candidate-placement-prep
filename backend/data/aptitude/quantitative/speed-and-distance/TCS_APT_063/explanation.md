# Question TCS_APT_063 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (36)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Speed and Distance

### Question
A train 150m long passes a pole in 15 seconds. Its speed in km/h is:

### Options
- (A) 36
- (B) 30
- (C) 40
- (D) 45

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Speed and Distance, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "36" is the valid solution.
