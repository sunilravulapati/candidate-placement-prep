# Question TCS_APT_436 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (2.25 km/h)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Speed and Distance

### Question
A man rows 10 km upstream in 100 min and downstream in 40 min. Speed of current?

### Options
- (A) 2.25 km/h
- (B) 3 km/h
- (C) 1.5 km/h
- (D) 2.5 km/h

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Speed and Distance, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "2.25 km/h" is the valid solution.
