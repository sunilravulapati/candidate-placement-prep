# Question TCS_APT_061 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (2.5 hours)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Speed and Distance

### Question
Two trains start from A and B, 300 km apart, toward each other at 50 km/h and 70 km/h. When do they meet?

### Options
- (A) 2.5 hours
- (B) 3 hours
- (C) 2 hours
- (D) 4 hours

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Speed and Distance, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "2.5 hours" is the valid solution.
