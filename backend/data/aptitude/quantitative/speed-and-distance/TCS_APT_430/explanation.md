# Question TCS_APT_430 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (8 sec)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Speed and Distance

### Question
Two trains 120m and 180m long approach each other at 60 and 90 km/h. Time to cross:

### Options
- (A) 8 sec
- (B) 6 sec
- (C) 10 sec
- (D) 12 sec

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Speed and Distance, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "8 sec" is the valid solution.
