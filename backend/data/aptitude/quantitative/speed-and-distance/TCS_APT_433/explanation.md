# Question TCS_APT_433 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (7.5 km/h)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Speed and Distance

### Question
A boat covers 30 km upstream in 6h and 30 km downstream in 3h. Boat speed in still water?

### Options
- (A) 7.5 km/h
- (B) 8 km/h
- (C) 6 km/h
- (D) 10 km/h

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Speed and Distance, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "7.5 km/h" is the valid solution.
