# Question TCS_APT_066 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (90 seconds)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Speed and Distance

### Question
A train 200m long passes a train 300m long going in the same direction at 60 km/h and 40 km/h. How long does it take?

### Options
- (A) 90 seconds
- (B) 100 seconds
- (C) 80 seconds
- (D) 120 seconds

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Speed and Distance, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "90 seconds" is the valid solution.
