# Question TCS_APT_073 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (9 hours)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Speed and Distance

### Question
Two persons A and B start at the same time from P and Q, 45 km apart, toward each other. A walks at 3 km/h and B at 2 km/h. When do they meet?

### Options
- (A) 9 hours
- (B) 10 hours
- (C) 8 hours
- (D) 12 hours

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Speed and Distance, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "9 hours" is the valid solution.
