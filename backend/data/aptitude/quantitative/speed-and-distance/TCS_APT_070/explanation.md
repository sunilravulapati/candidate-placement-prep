# Question TCS_APT_070 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (2 km/h)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Speed and Distance

### Question
A boat travels 24 km upstream in 6 hours and 24 km downstream in 4 hours. What is the speed of the stream?

### Options
- (A) 2 km/h
- (B) 1 km/h
- (C) 3 km/h
- (D) 4 km/h

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Speed and Distance, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "2 km/h" is the valid solution.
