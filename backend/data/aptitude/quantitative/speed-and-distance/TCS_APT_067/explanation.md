# Question TCS_APT_067 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (0.996 km)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Speed and Distance

### Question
Sound travels at 332 m/s. If lightning is seen 3 seconds before thunder, how far away is the lightning?

### Options
- (A) 0.996 km
- (B) 1 km
- (C) 1.5 km
- (D) 0.5 km

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Speed and Distance, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "0.996 km" is the valid solution.
