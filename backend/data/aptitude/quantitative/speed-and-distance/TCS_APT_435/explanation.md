# Question TCS_APT_435 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (2 hrs)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Speed and Distance

### Question
Two cities A and B are 300 km apart. A train from A travels at 70 km/h and another from B at 80 km/h toward each other. They meet after:

### Options
- (A) 2 hrs
- (B) 2.5 hrs
- (C) 3 hrs
- (D) 1.5 hrs

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Speed and Distance, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "2 hrs" is the valid solution.
