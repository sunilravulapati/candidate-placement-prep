# Question TCS_APT_071 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (4.5 hours)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Speed and Distance

### Question
If a train covers 360 km at 80 km/h, how long does the journey take?

### Options
- (A) 4.5 hours
- (B) 4 hours
- (C) 5 hours
- (D) 3.5 hours

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Speed and Distance, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "4.5 hours" is the valid solution.
