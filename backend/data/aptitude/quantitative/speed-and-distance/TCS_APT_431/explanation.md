# Question TCS_APT_431 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (66.67 km/h)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Speed and Distance

### Question
A car covers first 100 km at 50 km/h and next 100 km at 100 km/h. Average speed?

### Options
- (A) 66.67 km/h
- (B) 75 km/h
- (C) 70 km/h
- (D) 80 km/h

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Speed and Distance, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "66.67 km/h" is the valid solution.
