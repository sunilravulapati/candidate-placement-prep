# Question TCS_APT_432 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (60 min)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Speed and Distance

### Question
Walking at 3/4 of normal speed, a person is late by 20 min. Normal time is:

### Options
- (A) 60 min
- (B) 45 min
- (C) 80 min
- (D) 90 min

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Speed and Distance, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "60 min" is the valid solution.
