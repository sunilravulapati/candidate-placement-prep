# Question TCS_APT_068 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (3 minutes)**

### Topic
Numerical Ability > Speed and Distance

### Question
A thief is 100m ahead of a policeman. Both run, thief at 8 km/h and policeman at 10 km/h. When will the policeman catch the thief?

### Options
- (A) 3 minutes
- (B) 2 minutes
- (C) 5 minutes
- (D) 4 minutes

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Speed and Distance, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "3 minutes" is the valid solution.
