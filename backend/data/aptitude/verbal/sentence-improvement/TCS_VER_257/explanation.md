# Question TCS_VER_257 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (He is too weak to stand up.)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Sentence Improvement

### Question
Improve the underlined part of the sentence: 'He is too weak that he cannot stand up.'

### Options
- (A) He is too weak to stand up.
- (B) He is much weak to stand up.
- (C) He is very weak that he cannot stand up.
- (D) No improvement

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Sentence Improvement, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "He is too weak to stand up." is the valid solution.
