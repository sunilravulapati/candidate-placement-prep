# Question TCS_VER_269 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (accused of)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Sentence Improvement

### Question

- (B) accused with

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Sentence Improvement, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "accused of" is the valid solution.
