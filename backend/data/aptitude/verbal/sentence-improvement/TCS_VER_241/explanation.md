# Question TCS_VER_241 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (by Friday)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Sentence Improvement

### Question
Improve the underlined part of the sentence: 'The manager asked me to complete the work till Friday.'

### Options
- (A) until Friday
- (B) by Friday
- (C) up to Friday
- (D) No improvement

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Sentence Improvement, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "by Friday" is the valid solution.
