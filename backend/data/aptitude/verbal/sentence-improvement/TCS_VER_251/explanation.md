# Question TCS_VER_251 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (than the phone rang)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Sentence Improvement

### Question
Improve the underlined part of the sentence: 'No sooner did she enter when the phone rang.'

### Options
- (A) when the phone rang
- (B) than the phone rang
- (C) till the phone rang
- (D) No improvement

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Sentence Improvement, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "than the phone rang" is the valid solution.
