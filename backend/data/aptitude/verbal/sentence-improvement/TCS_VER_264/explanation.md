# Question TCS_VER_264 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option D (No improvement)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Sentence Improvement

### Question
Improve the underlined part of the sentence: 'He not only donated money but also gave his time.'

### Options
- (A) also gave his time
- (B) gave his time also
- (C) his time also gave
- (D) No improvement

### Explanation
Option (D) is the correct answer. In Sentence Improvement, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "No improvement" is the valid solution.
