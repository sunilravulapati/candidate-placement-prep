# Question TCS_VER_250 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (too tired to walk)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Sentence Improvement

### Question
Improve the underlined part of the sentence: 'He is too tired to not walk another mile.'

### Options
- (A) too tired to walk
- (B) very tired to not walk
- (C) too tired for walking
- (D) No improvement

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Sentence Improvement, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "too tired to walk" is the valid solution.
