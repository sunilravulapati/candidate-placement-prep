# Question TCS_VER_246 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (advised me to apply)**

Verbal Ability > Sentence Improvement

Option (A) is the correct answer. In Sentence Improvement, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "advised me to apply" is the valid solution.
