# Question TCS_VER_244 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (The news is)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Sentence Improvement

### Question
Improve the underlined part of the sentence: 'The news are very disturbing.'

### Options
- (A) The news is
- (B) The news were
- (C) A news is
- (D) No improvement

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Sentence Improvement, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "The news is" is the valid solution.
