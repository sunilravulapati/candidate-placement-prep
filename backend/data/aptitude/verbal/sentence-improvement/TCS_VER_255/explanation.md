# Question TCS_VER_255 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (would come)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Sentence Improvement

### Question
Improve the underlined part of the sentence: 'He did not know whether the train will come on time.'

### Options
- (A) would come
- (B) shall come
- (C) can come
- (D) No improvement

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Sentence Improvement, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "would come" is the valid solution.
