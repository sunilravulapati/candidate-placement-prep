# Question TCS_VER_261 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (acquitted of)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Sentence Improvement

### Question
Improve the underlined part of the sentence: 'He was acquitted for the crime.'

### Options
- (A) acquitted of
- (B) acquitted from
- (C) acquitted with
- (D) No improvement

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Sentence Improvement, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "acquitted of" is the valid solution.
