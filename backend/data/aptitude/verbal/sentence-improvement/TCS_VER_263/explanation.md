# Question TCS_VER_263 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (insisted on his paying)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Sentence Improvement

### Question
Improve the underlined part of the sentence: 'She insisted that he should pay the fine.'

### Options
- (A) insisted on his paying
- (B) insisted for him to pay
- (C) insisted him paying
- (D) No improvement

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Sentence Improvement, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "insisted on his paying" is the valid solution.
