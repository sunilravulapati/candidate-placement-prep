# Question TCS_VER_266 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (for two hours)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Sentence Improvement

### Question
Improve the underlined part of the sentence: 'I have been waiting for him since two hours.'

### Options
- (A) for two hours
- (B) since last two hours
- (C) at two hours
- (D) No improvement

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Sentence Improvement, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "for two hours" is the valid solution.
