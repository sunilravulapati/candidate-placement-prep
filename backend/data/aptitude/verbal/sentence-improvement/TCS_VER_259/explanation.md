# Question TCS_VER_259 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (on winning)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Sentence Improvement

### Question
Improve the underlined part of the sentence: 'She congratulated me for winning the prize.'

### Options
- (A) on winning
- (B) at winning
- (C) about winning
- (D) No improvement

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Sentence Improvement, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "on winning" is the valid solution.
