# Question TCS_VER_270 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option D (No improvement)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Sentence Improvement

### Question
Improve the underlined part of the sentence: 'He runs as fast as or even faster than his brother.'

### Options
- (A) as fast as or even faster
- (B) as fast or even faster than
- (C) faster or as fast as
- (D) No improvement

### Explanation
Option (D) is the correct answer. In Sentence Improvement, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "No improvement" is the valid solution.
