# Question TCS_VER_248 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Despite his illness)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Sentence Improvement

### Question

### Options

- (B) In spite his illness
- (C) Because of his illness

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Sentence Improvement, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Despite his illness" is the valid solution.
