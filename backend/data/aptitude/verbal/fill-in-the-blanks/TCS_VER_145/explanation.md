# Question TCS_VER_145 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (overwhelming)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Fill in the Blanks

### Question
The _____ evidence left no doubt about the suspect's guilt.

### Options
- (A) overwhelming
- (B) scant
- (C) limited
- (D) weak

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Fill in the Blanks, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "overwhelming" is the valid solution.
