# Question TCS_VER_144 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (deadlock)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Fill in the Blanks

### Question
The negotiations reached a _____ when neither party would compromise.

### Options
- (A) deadlock
- (B) agreement
- (C) resolution
- (D) conclusion

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Fill in the Blanks, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "deadlock" is the valid solution.
