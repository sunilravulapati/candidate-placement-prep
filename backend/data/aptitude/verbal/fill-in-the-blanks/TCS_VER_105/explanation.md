# Question TCS_VER_105 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (groundbreaking)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Fill in the Blanks

### Question
The scientist made a _____ discovery that changed modern medicine.

### Options
- (A) groundbreaking
- (B) trivial
- (C) minor
- (D) unremarkable

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Fill in the Blanks, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "groundbreaking" is the valid solution.
