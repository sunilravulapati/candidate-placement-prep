# Question TCS_VER_101 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (tedious)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Fill in the Blanks

### Question
The professor's lecture was so _____ that many students fell asleep.

### Options
- (A) tedious
- (B) engaging
- (C) stimulating
- (D) thrilling

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Fill in the Blanks, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "tedious" is the valid solution.
