# Question TCS_VER_134 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (methodical)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Fill in the Blanks

### Question
She was _____ in her approach and always followed the rules.

### Options
- (A) methodical
- (B) chaotic
- (C) random
- (D) careless

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Fill in the Blanks, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "methodical" is the valid solution.
