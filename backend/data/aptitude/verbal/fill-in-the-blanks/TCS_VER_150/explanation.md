# Question TCS_VER_150 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (intrepid)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Fill in the Blanks

### Question
The explorer was known for his _____ spirit and love of adventure.

### Options
- (A) intrepid
- (B) timid
- (C) cautious
- (D) fearful

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Fill in the Blanks, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "intrepid" is the valid solution.
