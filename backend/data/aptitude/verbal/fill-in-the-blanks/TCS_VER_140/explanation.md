# Question TCS_VER_140 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (dire)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Fill in the Blanks

### Question
The economy was in _____ condition after years of poor management.

### Options
- (A) dire
- (B) excellent
- (C) good
- (D) fine

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Fill in the Blanks, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "dire" is the valid solution.
