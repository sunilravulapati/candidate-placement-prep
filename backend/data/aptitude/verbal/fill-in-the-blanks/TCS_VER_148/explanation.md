# Question TCS_VER_148 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (altruistic)**

### Topic

### Question

### Options

- (B) selfish
- (C) greedy
- (D) self-centered

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Fill in the Blanks, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "altruistic" is the valid solution.
