# Question TCS_VER_102 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (perseverance)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Fill in the Blanks

### Question
She showed great _____ by finishing the project despite all obstacles.

### Options
- (A) perseverance
- (B) laziness
- (C) reluctance
- (D) hesitation

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Fill in the Blanks, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "perseverance" is the valid solution.
