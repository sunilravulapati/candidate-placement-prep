# Question TCS_VER_130 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (entrenched)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Fill in the Blanks

### Question
She had such _____ opinions that no one could change her mind.

### Options
- (A) entrenched
- (B) flexible
- (C) open
- (D) changing

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Fill in the Blanks, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "entrenched" is the valid solution.
