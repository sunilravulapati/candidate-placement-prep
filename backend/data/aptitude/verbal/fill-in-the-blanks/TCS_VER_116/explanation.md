# Question TCS_VER_116 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (maturity)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Fill in the Blanks

### Question
Despite his young age, he showed remarkable _____ in handling the crisis.

### Options
- (A) maturity
- (B) immaturity
- (C) inexperience
- (D) naivety

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Fill in the Blanks, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "maturity" is the valid solution.
