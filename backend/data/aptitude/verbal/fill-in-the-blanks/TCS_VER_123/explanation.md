# Question TCS_VER_123 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (overtaken)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Fill in the Blanks

### Question
The company was _____ by its competitors when it refused to innovate.

### Options
- (A) overtaken
- (B) supported
- (C) helped
- (D) aided

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Fill in the Blanks, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "overtaken" is the valid solution.
