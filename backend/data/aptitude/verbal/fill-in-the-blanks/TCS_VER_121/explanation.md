# Question TCS_VER_121 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (abandoned)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Fill in the Blanks

### Question
The peace talks were _____ after both parties failed to agree.

### Options
- (A) abandoned
- (B) concluded
- (C) finalized
- (D) successful

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Fill in the Blanks, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "abandoned" is the valid solution.
