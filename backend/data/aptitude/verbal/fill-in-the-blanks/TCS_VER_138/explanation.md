# Question TCS_VER_138 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (compelling)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Fill in the Blanks

### Question
The book was so _____ that she read it in one sitting.

### Options
- (A) compelling
- (B) boring
- (C) dull
- (D) ordinary

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Fill in the Blanks, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "compelling" is the valid solution.
