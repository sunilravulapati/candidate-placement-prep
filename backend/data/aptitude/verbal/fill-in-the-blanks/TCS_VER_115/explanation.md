# Question TCS_VER_115 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (baffled)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Fill in the Blanks

### Question

### Options
- (A) baffled
- (B) inspired

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Fill in the Blanks, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "baffled" is the valid solution.
