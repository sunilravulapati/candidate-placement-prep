# Question TCS_VER_127 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (authoritarian)**

### Topic

### Question

### Options

- (B) democratic

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Fill in the Blanks, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "authoritarian" is the valid solution.
