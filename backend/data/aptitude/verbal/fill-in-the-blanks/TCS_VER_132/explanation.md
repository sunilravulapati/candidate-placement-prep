# Question TCS_VER_132 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (incisive)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Fill in the Blanks

### Question
His _____ remarks showed that he had given much thought to the problem.

### Options
- (A) incisive
- (B) vague
- (C) unclear
- (D) shallow

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Fill in the Blanks, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "incisive" is the valid solution.
