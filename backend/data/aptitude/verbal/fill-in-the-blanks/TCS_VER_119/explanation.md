# Question TCS_VER_119 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (knack)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Fill in the Blanks

### Question
She had a _____ for solving puzzles and could finish them in minutes.

### Options
- (A) knack
- (B) aversion
- (C) dislike
- (D) fear

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Fill in the Blanks, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "knack" is the valid solution.
