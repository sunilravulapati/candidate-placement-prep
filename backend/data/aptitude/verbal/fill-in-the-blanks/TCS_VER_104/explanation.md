# Question TCS_VER_104 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (derogatory)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Fill in the Blanks

### Question
His _____ remarks offended many people in the audience.

### Options
- (A) derogatory
- (B) kind
- (C) polite
- (D) flattering

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Fill in the Blanks, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "derogatory" is the valid solution.
