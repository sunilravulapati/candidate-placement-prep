# Question TCS_VER_113 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (prudent)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Fill in the Blanks

### Question

### Options

- (B) rash

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Fill in the Blanks, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "prudent" is the valid solution.
