# Question TCS_VER_106 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (meticulous)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Fill in the Blanks

### Question
She was _____ about her diet, avoiding sugar and processed foods.

### Options
- (A) meticulous
- (B) careless
- (C) relaxed
- (D) indifferent

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Fill in the Blanks, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "meticulous" is the valid solution.
