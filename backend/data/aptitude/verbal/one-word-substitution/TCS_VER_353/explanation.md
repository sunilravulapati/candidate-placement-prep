# Question TCS_VER_353 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option C (Itinerant)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > One Word Substitution

### Question
Find the one word substitution for: One who travels from place to place

### Options
- (A) Nomad
- (B) Vagabond
- (C) Itinerant
- (D) All of above

### Explanation
Option (C) is the correct answer. In One Word Substitution, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Itinerant" is the valid solution.
