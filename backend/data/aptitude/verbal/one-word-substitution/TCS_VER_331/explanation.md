# Question TCS_VER_331 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Incorrigible)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > One Word Substitution

### Question
Find the one word substitution for: A person who cannot be corrected

### Options
- (A) Incorrigible
- (B) Gullible
- (C) Impeccable
- (D) Insolent

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In One Word Substitution, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Incorrigible" is the valid solution.
