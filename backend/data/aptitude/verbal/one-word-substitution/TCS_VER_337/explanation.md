# Question TCS_VER_337 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Insomniac)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > One Word Substitution

### Question
Find the one word substitution for: A person who is unable to sleep

### Options
- (A) Insomniac
- (B) Somnambulant
- (C) Lethargic
- (D) Hypnotic

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In One Word Substitution, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Insomniac" is the valid solution.
