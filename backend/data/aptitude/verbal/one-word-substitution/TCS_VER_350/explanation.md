# Question TCS_VER_350 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Ambidextrous)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > One Word Substitution

### Question
Find the one word substitution for: One who can use both hands equally well

### Options
- (A) Ambidextrous
- (B) Ambiguous
- (C) Versatile
- (D) Bilateral

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In One Word Substitution, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Ambidextrous" is the valid solution.
