# Question TCS_VER_207 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (P-Q-R-S)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Para Jumbles

### Question
P: The Amazon rainforest is often called the lungs of the Earth. Q: It produces approximately 20% of the world's oxygen. R: Rapid deforestation threatens not only wildlife but global climate stability. S: Protecting it must be a global priority. Arrange in logical order:

### Options
- (A) P-Q-R-S
- (B) R-P-Q-S
- (C) Q-P-R-S
- (D) P-R-Q-S

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Para Jumbles, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "P-Q-R-S" is the valid solution.
