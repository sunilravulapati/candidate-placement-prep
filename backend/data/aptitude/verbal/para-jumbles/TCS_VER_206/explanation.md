# Question TCS_VER_206 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (P-Q-R-S)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Para Jumbles

### Question
P: Meditation has been practiced for thousands of years. Q: Modern science now confirms its benefits for mental health. R: It reduces stress, improves focus, and promotes emotional well-being. S: Even a few minutes of daily meditation can make a noticeable difference. Arrange in logical order:

### Options
- (A) P-Q-R-S
- (B) Q-P-R-S
- (C) R-P-Q-S
- (D) S-Q-P-R

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Para Jumbles, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "P-Q-R-S" is the valid solution.
