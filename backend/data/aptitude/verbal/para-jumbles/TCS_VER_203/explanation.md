# Question TCS_VER_203 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (P-Q-R-S)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Para Jumbles

### Question
P: Water is the most precious resource on Earth. Q: Without it, no life can survive. R: Yet, billions of people lack access to clean drinking water. S: Conservation and responsible usage are therefore critical. Arrange in logical order:

### Options
- (A) P-Q-R-S
- (B) R-P-Q-S
- (C) Q-P-R-S
- (D) P-R-Q-S

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Para Jumbles, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "P-Q-R-S" is the valid solution.
