# Question TCS_VER_202 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (P-S-R-Q)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Para Jumbles

### Question
P: Exercise is essential for maintaining good health. Q: Even a 30-minute daily walk can significantly reduce the risk of chronic diseases. R: Many people, however, lead sedentary lifestyles due to desk jobs. S: It strengthens the heart, improves mood, and increases longevity. Arrange in logical order:

### Options
- (A) P-S-R-Q
- (B) R-P-S-Q
- (C) P-Q-S-R
- (D) S-R-P-Q

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Para Jumbles, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "P-S-R-Q" is the valid solution.
