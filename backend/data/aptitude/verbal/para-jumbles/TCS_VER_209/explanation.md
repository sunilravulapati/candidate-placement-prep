# Question TCS_VER_209 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (P-Q-R-S)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Para Jumbles

### Question
P: Reading regularly enhances vocabulary and critical thinking. Q: It also reduces stress by providing an escape from daily pressures. R: Children who read from a young age develop stronger academic skills. S: Encouraging a reading habit is one of the greatest gifts parents can give. Arrange in logical order:

### Options
- (A) R-P-Q-S
- (B) P-Q-R-S
- (C) S-P-Q-R
- (D) Q-R-P-S

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Para Jumbles, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "P-Q-R-S" is the valid solution.
