# Question TCS_VER_381 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (failed)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Cloze Test

### Question
Climate negotiations have repeatedly _____ (1) to produce binding commitments. While leaders acknowledge the _____ (2) of the crisis, domestic politics often _____ (3) international cooperation. SELECT WORD FOR BLANK (1):

### Options
- (A) failed
- (B) succeeded
- (C) managed
- (D) helped

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Cloze Test, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "failed" is the valid solution.
