# Question TCS_VER_383 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (considerably)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Cloze Test

### Question
Mental health awareness has grown _____ (1) in recent years. Society is slowly _____ (2) the stigma that once surrounded mental illness, making it easier for individuals to _____ (3) help. SELECT WORD FOR BLANK (1):

### Options
- (A) considerably
- (B) negligibly
- (C) minimally
- (D) barely

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Cloze Test, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "considerably" is the valid solution.
