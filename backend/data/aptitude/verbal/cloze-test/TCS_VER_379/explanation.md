# Question TCS_VER_379 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (enhance)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Cloze Test

### Question
Reading fiction has been shown to _____ (1) empathy. When readers _____ (2) themselves in the lives of characters, they develop a deeper understanding of perspectives different from their _____ (3). SELECT WORD FOR BLANK (1):

### Options
- (A) enhance
- (B) reduce
- (C) damage
- (D) weaken

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Cloze Test, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "enhance" is the valid solution.
