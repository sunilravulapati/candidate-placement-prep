# Question TCS_VER_376 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (demonstrated)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Cloze Test

### Question
Scientists have long held the idea that humans alone solve complex problems. Recent studies have _____ (2) that crows and elephants also exhibit remarkable intelligence. SELECT WORD FOR BLANK (2):

### Options
- (A) demonstrated
- (B) hidden
- (C) denied
- (D) ignored

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Cloze Test, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "demonstrated" is the valid solution.
