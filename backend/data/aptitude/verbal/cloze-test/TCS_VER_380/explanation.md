# Question TCS_VER_380 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (immerse)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Cloze Test

### Question
Reading fiction enhances empathy. When readers _____ (2) themselves in the lives of characters, they understand different perspectives. SELECT WORD FOR BLANK (2):

### Options
- (A) immerse
- (B) isolate
- (C) distance
- (D) remove

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Cloze Test, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "immerse" is the valid solution.
