# Question TCS_VER_370 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (transforming)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Cloze Test

### Question
In today's world, technology has become _____ of our lives. From smartphones to smart homes, devices are _____ (2) every aspect of human existence. While this brings tremendous convenience, it also raises concerns about privacy and mental health. SELECT WORD FOR BLANK (2):

### Options
- (A) transforming
- (B) destroying
- (C) ignoring
- (D) eliminating

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Cloze Test, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "transforming" is the valid solution.
