Verbal Ability > Cloze Test

### Question
Scientists have long _____ (1) the idea that humans are the only species capable of complex problem-solving. Recent studies, however, have _____ (2) that crows, dolphins, and elephants also exhibit remarkable _____ (3). SELECT WORD FOR BLANK (1):

### Options

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Cloze Test, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "held" is the valid solution.
