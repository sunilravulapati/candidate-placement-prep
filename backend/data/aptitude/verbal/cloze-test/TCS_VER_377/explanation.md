# Question TCS_VER_377 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (transition)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Cloze Test

### Question
The global economy is undergoing a major _____ (1). Automation and artificial intelligence are _____ (2) traditional jobs, forcing workers to _____ (3) new skills to remain relevant. SELECT WORD FOR BLANK (1):

### Options
- (A) transition
- (B) failure
- (C) stagnation
- (D) decline

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Cloze Test, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "transition" is the valid solution.
