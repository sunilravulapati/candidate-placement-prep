# Question TCS_VER_373 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (dominated)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Cloze Test

### Question
The city had grown rapidly over the past decade. Skyscrapers now _____ (1) the skyline where rice fields once _____ (2). The transformation was _____ (3), but it came with the cost of displacing thousands of farmers. SELECT WORD FOR BLANK (1):

### Options
- (A) dominated
- (B) decorated
- (C) disturbed
- (D) blocked

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Cloze Test, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "dominated" is the valid solution.
