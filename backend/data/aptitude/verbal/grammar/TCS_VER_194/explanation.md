# Question TCS_VER_194 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (She was tired; however, she kept working.)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Grammar

### Question
Which sentence correctly uses a semicolon?

### Options
- (A) She was tired; but she kept working.
- (B) She was tired; however, she kept working.
- (C) She was tired; and she kept working.
- (D) She was tired; yet, she kept working.

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Grammar, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "She was tired; however, she kept working." is the valid solution.
