# Question TCS_VER_198 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (She is the best student in the class.)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Grammar

### Question
Identify the correct use of articles:

### Options
- (A) She is best student in the class.
- (B) She is the best student in the class.
- (C) She is a best student in class.
- (D) She is best student in a class.

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Grammar, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "She is the best student in the class." is the valid solution.
