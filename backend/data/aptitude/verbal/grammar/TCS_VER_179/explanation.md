# Question TCS_VER_179 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (The children's toys were scattered.)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Grammar

### Question
Which sentence uses the apostrophe correctly?

### Options
- (A) The childrens' toys were scattered.
- (B) The children's toys were scattered.
- (C) The childrens toys' were scattered.
- (D) The children toys were scattered.

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Grammar, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "The children's toys were scattered." is the valid solution.
