# Question TCS_VER_192 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (Whom did you speak to?)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Grammar

### Question
Identify the sentence with correct use of 'who' and 'whom':

### Options
- (A) Who did you speak to?
- (B) Whom did you speak to?
- (C) To who did you speak?
- (D) Whom spoke to you?

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Grammar, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Whom did you speak to?" is the valid solution.
