# Question TCS_VER_173 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (The team is playing well today.)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Grammar

### Question
Identify the grammatically correct sentence:

### Options
- (A) The team are playing good today.
- (B) The team is playing well today.
- (C) The team is playing good today.
- (D) The team are playing well today.

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Grammar, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "The team is playing well today." is the valid solution.
