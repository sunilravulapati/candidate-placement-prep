# Question TCS_VER_199 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (The teacher told the student, 'You are wrong.')**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Grammar

### Question
Choose the sentence with correct pronoun reference:

### Options
- (A) The teacher told the student that he was wrong.
- (B) The teacher told the student, 'You are wrong.'
- (C) He was told by the teacher he was wrong.
- (D) The teacher told him he was wrong to student.

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Grammar, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "The teacher told the student, 'You are wrong.'" is the valid solution.
