# Question TCS_VER_189 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (Do you know where he lives?)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Grammar

### Question
Which is the correctly formed question?

### Options
- (A) Do you know where does he live?
- (B) Do you know where he lives?
- (C) Do you know where he is living?
- (D) Do you know where is he living?

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Grammar, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Do you know where he lives?" is the valid solution.
