# Question TCS_VER_187 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (She likes reading, swimming, and jogging.)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Grammar

### Question
Identify the sentence with correct parallel structure:

### Options
- (A) She likes reading, to swim, and jogging.
- (B) She likes reading, swimming, and jogging.
- (C) She likes to read, swim, and jogging.
- (D) She likes to read, swimming, and to jog.

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Grammar, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "She likes reading, swimming, and jogging." is the valid solution.
