# Question TCS_VER_175 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option C (He has gone to the market.)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Grammar

### Question
Which sentence uses the correct form of the verb?

### Options
- (A) He has went to the market.
- (B) He have gone to the market.
- (C) He has gone to the market.
- (D) He have went to the market.

### Explanation
Option (C) is the correct answer. In Grammar, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "He has gone to the market." is the valid solution.
