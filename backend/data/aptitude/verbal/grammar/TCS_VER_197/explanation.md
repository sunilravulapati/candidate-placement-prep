# Question TCS_VER_197 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (She will have completed the project by tomorrow.)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Grammar

### Question
Which sentence is in the future perfect tense?

### Options
- (A) She will complete the project.
- (B) She will have completed the project by tomorrow.
- (C) She is completing the project.
- (D) She has completed the project.

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Grammar, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "She will have completed the project by tomorrow." is the valid solution.
