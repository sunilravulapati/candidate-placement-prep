# Question TCS_VER_171 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (Neither of the boys has done his homework.)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Grammar

### Question
Identify the correct sentence:

### Options
- (A) Neither of the boys have done their homework.
- (B) Neither of the boys has done his homework.
- (C) Neither of the boys has done their homeworks.
- (D) Neither of the boys have done his homework.

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Grammar, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Neither of the boys has done his homework." is the valid solution.
