# Question TCS_VER_181 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (Fewer people attended the event this year.)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Grammar

### Question
Choose the sentence with correct use of 'fewer' and 'less':

### Options
- (A) Less people attended the event this year.
- (B) Fewer people attended the event this year.
- (C) Less peoples attended the event this year.
- (D) Fewer amount of people attended.

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Grammar, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Fewer people attended the event this year." is the valid solution.
