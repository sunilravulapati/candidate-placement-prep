# Question TCS_VER_184 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Walking to school, the rain started.)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Grammar

### Question
Which sentence contains a dangling modifier?

### Options
- (A) Walking to school, the rain started.
- (B) While walking to school, I got wet in the rain.
- (C) Walking to school, she got wet in the rain.
- (D) She was walking to school when it rained.

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Grammar, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Walking to school, the rain started." is the valid solution.
