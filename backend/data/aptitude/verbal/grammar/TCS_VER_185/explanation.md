# Question TCS_VER_185 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (I wish I were taller than him.)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Grammar

### Question
Choose the grammatically correct sentence:

### Options
- (A) I wish I was taller than him.
- (B) I wish I were taller than him.
- (C) I wish I am taller than him.
- (D) I wish I be taller than him.

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Grammar, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "I wish I were taller than him." is the valid solution.
