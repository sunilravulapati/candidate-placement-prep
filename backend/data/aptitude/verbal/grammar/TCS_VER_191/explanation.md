# Question TCS_VER_191 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (The boys' bicycle is red.)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Grammar

### Question
Choose the correctly written sentence:

### Options
- (A) The boys' bicycle is red.
- (B) The boys bicycle is red.
- (C) The boy's bicycles is red.
- (D) The boys bicycles is red.

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Grammar, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "The boys' bicycle is red." is the valid solution.
