# Question TCS_VER_177 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (Between you and me, this is wrong.)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Grammar

### Question
Choose the sentence that is grammatically correct:

### Options
- (A) Between you and I, this is wrong.
- (B) Between you and me, this is wrong.
- (C) Between you and I, this is right.
- (D) Between me and you, this is wrong.

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Grammar, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Between you and me, this is wrong." is the valid solution.
