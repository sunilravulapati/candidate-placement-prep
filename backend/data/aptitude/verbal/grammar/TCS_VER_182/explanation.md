# Question TCS_VER_182 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (A letter is written by her.)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Grammar

### Question
Identify the passive voice sentence:

### Options
- (A) She writes a letter.
- (B) A letter is written by her.
- (C) She is writing a letter.
- (D) She wrote a letter.

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Grammar, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "A letter is written by her." is the valid solution.
