# Question TCS_VER_193 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (Each of the students has submitted his or her form.)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Grammar

### Question
Choose the grammatically correct option:

### Options
- (A) Each of the students have submitted their forms.
- (B) Each of the students has submitted his or her form.
- (C) Each of the student has submitted their form.
- (D) Each students has submitted their forms.

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Grammar, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Each of the students has submitted his or her form." is the valid solution.
