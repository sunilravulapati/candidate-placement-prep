# Question TCS_VER_218 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (It is your responsibility to act)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Idioms and Phrases

### Question
What does 'the ball is in your court' mean?

### Options
- (A) It is your responsibility to act
- (B) Play a sport
- (C) Take a break
- (D) Ask for help

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Idioms and Phrases, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "It is your responsibility to act" is the valid solution.
