# Question TCS_VER_225 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Both parties are responsible for a conflict)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Idioms and Phrases

### Question
What does 'it takes two to tango' mean?

### Options
- (A) Both parties are responsible for a conflict
- (B) Dance alone
- (C) Need music
- (D) One person is wrong

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Idioms and Phrases, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Both parties are responsible for a conflict" is the valid solution.
