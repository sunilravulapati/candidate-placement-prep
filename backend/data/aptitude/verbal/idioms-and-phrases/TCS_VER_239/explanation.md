# Question TCS_VER_239 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (A dangerous person disguised as harmless)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Idioms and Phrases

### Question
What does 'wolf in sheep's clothing' mean?

### Options
- (A) A dangerous person disguised as harmless
- (B) An animal wearing clothes
- (C) A friendly stranger
- (D) An honest person

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Idioms and Phrases, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "A dangerous person disguised as harmless" is the valid solution.
