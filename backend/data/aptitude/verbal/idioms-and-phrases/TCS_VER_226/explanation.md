# Question TCS_VER_226 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Do not disturb a situation that could cause trouble)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Idioms and Phrases

### Question
What does 'let sleeping dogs lie' mean?

### Options
- (A) Do not disturb a situation that could cause trouble
- (B) Let dogs rest
- (C) Avoid noise
- (D) Stay calm

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Idioms and Phrases, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Do not disturb a situation that could cause trouble" is the valid solution.
