# Question TCS_VER_214 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Be very expensive)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Idioms and Phrases

### Question
What does 'cost an arm and a leg' mean?

### Options
- (A) Be very expensive
- (B) Require physical effort
- (C) Cause injury
- (D) Need surgery

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Idioms and Phrases, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Be very expensive" is the valid solution.
