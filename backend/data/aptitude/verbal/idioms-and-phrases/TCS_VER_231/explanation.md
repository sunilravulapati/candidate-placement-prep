# Question TCS_VER_231 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Refuse to take a side in a dispute)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Idioms and Phrases

### Question

### Options
- (A) Refuse to take a side in a dispute
- (B) Rest outdoors
- (C) Guard a property
- (D) Climb a fence

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Idioms and Phrases, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Refuse to take a side in a dispute" is the valid solution.
