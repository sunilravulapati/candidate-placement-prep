# Question TCS_VER_220 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Take on more than you can handle)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Idioms and Phrases

### Question
What does 'bite more than you can chew' mean?

### Options
- (A) Take on more than you can handle
- (B) Eat excessively
- (C) Be greedy
- (D) Avoid responsibilities

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Idioms and Phrases, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Take on more than you can handle" is the valid solution.
