# Question TCS_VER_233 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Enjoy two advantages at the same time)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Idioms and Phrases

### Question
What does 'the best of both worlds' mean?

### Options
- (A) Enjoy two advantages at the same time
- (B) Travel worldwide
- (C) Win every competition
- (D) Be the best player

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Idioms and Phrases, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Enjoy two advantages at the same time" is the valid solution.
