# Question TCS_VER_238 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Express emotions openly)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Idioms and Phrases

### Question

### Options

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Idioms and Phrases, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Express emotions openly" is the valid solution.
