# Question TCS_VER_240 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Don't judge by appearances alone)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Idioms and Phrases

### Question
What does 'you can't judge a book by its cover' mean?

### Options
- (A) Don't judge by appearances alone
- (B) Never read books
- (C) Avoid assumptions
- (D) Always judge quickly

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Idioms and Phrases, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Don't judge by appearances alone" is the valid solution.
