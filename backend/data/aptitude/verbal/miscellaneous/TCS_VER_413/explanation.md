# Question TCS_VER_413 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (She had finished the work before he arrived.)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Miscellaneous

### Question
Which sentence is in the pluperfect (past perfect) tense?

### Options
- (A) She had finished the work before he arrived.
- (B) She finished the work before he arrived.
- (C) She has finished the work before he arrived.
- (D) She was finishing the work when he arrived.

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Miscellaneous, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "She had finished the work before he arrived." is the valid solution.
