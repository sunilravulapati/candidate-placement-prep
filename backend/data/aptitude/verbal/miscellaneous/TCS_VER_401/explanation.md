# Question TCS_VER_401 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (from)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Miscellaneous

### Question
Choose the correct preposition: 'She was absent _____ the meeting.'

### Options
- (A) from
- (B) at
- (C) in
- (D) on

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Miscellaneous, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "from" is the valid solution.
