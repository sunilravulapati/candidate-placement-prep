# Question TCS_VER_411 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (She enjoys swimming.)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Miscellaneous

### Question
Which sentence uses the gerund correctly?

### Options
- (A) She enjoys to swim.
- (B) She enjoys swimming.
- (C) She enjoys for swimming.
- (D) She enjoys swims.

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Miscellaneous, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "She enjoys swimming." is the valid solution.
