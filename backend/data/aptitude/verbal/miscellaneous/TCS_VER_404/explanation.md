# Question TCS_VER_404 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (The rain affected our plans.)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Miscellaneous

### Question
Which sentence uses 'affect' correctly?

### Options
- (A) The rain effected our plans.
- (B) The rain affected our plans.
- (C) The rain had a big affect on us.
- (D) He was unaffect by the news.

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Miscellaneous, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "The rain affected our plans." is the valid solution.
