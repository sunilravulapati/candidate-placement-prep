# Question TCS_VER_400 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (of)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Miscellaneous

### Question
Choose the correct preposition: 'He died _____ cancer.'

### Options
- (A) from
- (B) of
- (C) with
- (D) by

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Miscellaneous, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "of" is the valid solution.
