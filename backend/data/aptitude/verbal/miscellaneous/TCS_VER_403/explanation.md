# Question TCS_VER_403 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (He has worked here since 2020.)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Miscellaneous

### Question
Which sentence uses 'since' correctly?

### Options
- (A) He has worked here since two years.
- (B) He has worked here since 2020.
- (C) He is working since yesterday.
- (D) He worked since Monday.

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Miscellaneous, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "He has worked here since 2020." is the valid solution.
