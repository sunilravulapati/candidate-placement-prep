# Question TCS_VER_405 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (The principal of the school arrived.)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Miscellaneous

### Question
Which sentence uses 'principal' correctly?

### Options
- (A) She was the principle reason for success.
- (B) The principal of the school arrived.
- (C) We must follow the principals.
- (D) He abided by a principle reason.

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Miscellaneous, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "The principal of the school arrived." is the valid solution.
