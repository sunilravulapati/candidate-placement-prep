# Question TCS_VER_407 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (He said that he was happy.)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Miscellaneous

### Question
Which is the correct reported speech for: He said, 'I am happy'?

### Options
- (A) He said that he is happy.
- (B) He said that he was happy.
- (C) He said that I am happy.
- (D) He told he was happy.

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Miscellaneous, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "He said that he was happy." is the valid solution.
