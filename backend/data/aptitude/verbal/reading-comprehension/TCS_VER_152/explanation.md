# Question TCS_VER_152 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Reduction in urbanization)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Reading Comprehension

### Question
PASSAGE: The Industrial Revolution, which began in Britain in the late 18th century, fundamentally transformed the world. It mark... Q: Which of the following was NOT a consequence of the Industrial Revolution?

### Options
- (A) Reduction in urbanization
- (B) Poor working conditions
- (C) Child labor
- (D) Environmental pollution

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Reading Comprehension, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Reduction in urbanization" is the valid solution.
