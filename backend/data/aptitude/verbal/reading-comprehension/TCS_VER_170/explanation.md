# Question TCS_VER_170 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (That the brain is fixed after childhood)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Reading Comprehension

### Question
PASSAGE: The human brain is arguably the most complex structure in the known universe. Comprising approximately 86 billion neuron... Q: Which old notion does neuroplasticity challenge?

### Options
- (A) That the brain is fixed after childhood
- (B) That the brain grows throughout life
- (C) That neurons multiply continuously
- (D) That synapses are temporary

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Reading Comprehension, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "That the brain is fixed after childhood" is the valid solution.
