# Question TCS_VER_153 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (A new working class)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Reading Comprehension

### Question
PASSAGE: The Industrial Revolution, which began in Britain in the late 18th century, fundamentally transformed the world. It mark... Q: What social development emerged as a direct result of the Industrial Revolution?

### Options
- (A) A new working class
- (B) Decline of cities
- (C) Return to farming
- (D) Fewer social inequalities

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Reading Comprehension, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "A new working class" is the valid solution.
