# Question TCS_VER_154 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Reform movements and labor unions)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Reading Comprehension

### Question
PASSAGE: The Industrial Revolution, which began in Britain in the late 18th century, fundamentally transformed the world. It mark... Q: What eventually helped improve working conditions?

### Options
- (A) Reform movements and labor unions
- (B) Industrial revolution itself
- (C) Factory owners' goodwill
- (D) Government bans on industry

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Reading Comprehension, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Reform movements and labor unions" is the valid solution.
