# Question TCS_VER_169 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Consolidates memories and removes metabolic waste)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Reading Comprehension

### Question
PASSAGE: The human brain is arguably the most complex structure in the known universe. Comprising approximately 86 billion neuron... Q: What does the brain do during sleep according to the passage?

### Options
- (A) Consolidates memories and removes metabolic waste
- (B) Completely shuts down
- (C) Only controls breathing
- (D) Stops all activity

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Reading Comprehension, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Consolidates memories and removes metabolic waste" is the valid solution.
