# Question TCS_VER_166 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (86 billion)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Reading Comprehension

### Question
PASSAGE: The human brain is arguably the most complex structure in the known universe. Comprising approximately 86 billion neuron... Q: Approximately how many neurons does the human brain contain?

### Options
- (A) 86 billion
- (B) 86 million
- (C) 860 billion
- (D) 8.6 trillion

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Reading Comprehension, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "86 billion" is the valid solution.
