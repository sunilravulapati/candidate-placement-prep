# Question TCS_VER_476 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (The sun has risen.)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Vocabulary

### Question
Which sentence uses 'risen' correctly?

### Options
- (A) The sun has risen.
- (B) The sun has rose.
- (C) The sun had rose.
- (D) The sun was risen.

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Vocabulary, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "The sun has risen." is the valid solution.
