# Question TCS_VER_451 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (will have been working)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Vocabulary

### Question
Choose the correct form: He _____ here for five years by next June.

### Options
- (A) will be working
- (B) will have been working
- (C) has been working
- (D) was working

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Vocabulary, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "will have been working" is the valid solution.
