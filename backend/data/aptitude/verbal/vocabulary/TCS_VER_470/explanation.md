# Question TCS_VER_470 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (The door is opened by him every day.)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Vocabulary

### Question
Choose the correct sentence: Passive voice of 'He opens the door every day.'

### Options
- (A) The door is opened by him every day.
- (B) The door was opened by him.
- (C) The door has been opened by him.
- (D) The door opens every day.

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Vocabulary, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "The door is opened by him every day." is the valid solution.
