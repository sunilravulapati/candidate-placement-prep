# Question TCS_VER_486 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (There are fewer students today.)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Vocabulary

### Question
Which sentence uses the correct form of 'less' vs 'fewer'?

### Options
- (A) There are less students today.
- (B) There are fewer students today.
- (C) There are more less students.
- (D) There are fewer amount.

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Vocabulary, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "There are fewer students today." is the valid solution.
