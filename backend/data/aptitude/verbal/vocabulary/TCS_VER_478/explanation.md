# Question TCS_VER_478 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (To fail or be defeated)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Vocabulary

### Question
Choose the meaning of the phrase 'to bite the dust':

### Options
- (A) To fail or be defeated
- (B) To eat soil
- (C) To fall asleep
- (D) To become tired

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Vocabulary, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "To fail or be defeated" is the valid solution.
