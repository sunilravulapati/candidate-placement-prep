# Question TCS_VER_493 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option B (Neither he nor she is going.)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Vocabulary

### Question
Choose the correctly formed sentence:

### Options
- (A) Neither he nor she are going.
- (B) Neither he nor she is going.
- (C) Neither he nor she were going.
- (D) Neither he nor she have gone.

### Explanation
Option (B) is the correct answer. In Vocabulary, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Neither he nor she is going." is the valid solution.
