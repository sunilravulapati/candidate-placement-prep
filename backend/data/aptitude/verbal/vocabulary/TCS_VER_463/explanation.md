# Question TCS_VER_463 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (She wrote a letter.)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Vocabulary

### Question
Choose the sentence in active voice: 'A letter was written by her.'

### Options
- (A) She wrote a letter.
- (B) A letter she wrote.
- (C) Letter was written.
- (D) She has written letters.

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Vocabulary, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "She wrote a letter." is the valid solution.
