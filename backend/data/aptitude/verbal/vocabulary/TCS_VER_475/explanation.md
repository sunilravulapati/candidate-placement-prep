**Correct Answer: Option A (She laid the book on the table yesterday.)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Vocabulary

### Question

### Options

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Vocabulary, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "She laid the book on the table yesterday." is the valid solution.
