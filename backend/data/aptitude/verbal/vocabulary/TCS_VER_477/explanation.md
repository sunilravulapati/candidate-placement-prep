# Question TCS_VER_477 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (He only eats vegetables on Mondays.)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Vocabulary

### Question
Choose the sentence with a misplaced modifier:

### Options
- (A) He only eats vegetables on Mondays.
- (B) On Mondays, he eats only vegetables.
- (C) He eats only vegetables.
- (D) He eats vegetables only on Mondays.

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Vocabulary, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "He only eats vegetables on Mondays." is the valid solution.
