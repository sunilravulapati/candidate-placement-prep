# Question TCS_VER_481 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Immediately without delay)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Vocabulary

### Question
Choose the meaning of 'at the drop of a hat':

### Options
- (A) Immediately without delay
- (B) Accidentally
- (C) With great effort
- (D) Slowly

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Vocabulary, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Immediately without delay" is the valid solution.
