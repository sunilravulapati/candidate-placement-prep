# Question TCS_VER_474 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (would)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Vocabulary

### Question
Choose the correct form: 'I _____ rather stay home than go out.'

### Options
- (A) would
- (B) should
- (C) could
- (D) might

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Vocabulary, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "would" is the valid solution.
