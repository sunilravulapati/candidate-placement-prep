# Question TCS_VER_480 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Accomplish two things with one effort)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Vocabulary

### Question
Choose the meaning of the phrase 'kill two birds with one stone':

### Options
- (A) Accomplish two things with one effort
- (B) Be violent
- (C) Be efficient
- (D) Waste time

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Vocabulary, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Accomplish two things with one effort" is the valid solution.
