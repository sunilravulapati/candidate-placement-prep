# Question TCS_VER_479 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (To become nervous or afraid)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Vocabulary

### Question
Choose the meaning of the phrase 'to get cold feet':

### Options
- (A) To become nervous or afraid
- (B) To feel physically cold
- (C) To lose a competition
- (D) To stop working

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Vocabulary, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "To become nervous or afraid" is the valid solution.
