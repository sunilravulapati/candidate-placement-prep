# Question TCS_VER_435 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (Overworking and exhausting oneself)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Vocabulary

### Question
What does the idiom 'burning the candle at both ends' mean?

### Options
- (A) Overworking and exhausting oneself
- (B) Being very productive
- (C) Wasting resources
- (D) Enjoying life

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Vocabulary, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "Overworking and exhausting oneself" is the valid solution.
