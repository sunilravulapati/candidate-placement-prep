# Question TCS_VER_473 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (She wants to quickly finish the work.)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Vocabulary

### Question
Which sentence contains a split infinitive?

### Options
- (A) She wants to quickly finish the work.
- (B) She quickly wants to finish the work.
- (C) She wants to finish the work quickly.
- (D) She quickly finishes the work.

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Vocabulary, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "She wants to quickly finish the work." is the valid solution.
