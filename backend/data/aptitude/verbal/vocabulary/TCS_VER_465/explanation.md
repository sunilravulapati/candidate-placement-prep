# Question TCS_VER_465 — Explanation

**Correct Answer: Option A (isn't she)**

### Topic
Verbal Ability > Vocabulary

### Question
Choose the correctly formed question tag: 'She is intelligent, ___?'

### Options
- (A) isn't she
- (B) is she
- (C) was she
- (D) wasn't she

### Explanation
Option (A) is the correct answer. In Vocabulary, evaluating the given problem conditions confirms that "isn't she" is the valid solution.
